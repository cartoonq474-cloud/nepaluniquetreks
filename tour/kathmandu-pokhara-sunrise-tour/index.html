Best Time to Visit Kathmandu Pokhara Sunrise Tour

The Kathmandu Pokhara Sunrise Tour can be enjoyed throughout the year, but the experience varies depending on weather, visibility, and travel comfort. Choosing the right season helps you get the best views of the Himalayas, clear sunrise moments, and smooth travel conditions for your Tour in Nepal.

Spring Season (March to May) – Best Overall Time

Spring is one of the most popular times for Nepal Tour, Trekking in Nepal, and Sightseeing in Nepal.

  • Clear skies with excellent Himalayan visibility
  • Blooming rhododendrons and green landscapes
  • Ideal weather for Kathmandu Valley Sightseeing and Pokhara Valley Tour
  • Perfect sunrise views from Nagarkot and Sarangkot

Best choice for Family Tour in Nepal, Honeymoon Tour in Nepal, and photography lovers

Autumn Season (September to November) – Peak Tourist Season

Autumn is widely considered the best time to visit Nepal for any kind of UNESCO World Heritage Tour in Nepal or Himalayan experience.

  • Crystal clear mountain views after the monsoon
  • Stable weather and comfortable temperatures
  • Best sunrise visibility from Nagarkot and Sarangkot
  • Ideal conditions for Day Tours in Nepal and cultural sightseeing

Highly recommended for Visit Nepal, Pokhara Tour, and Annapurna Tour experiences

Winter Season (December to February) – Calm & Clear Views

Winter offers peaceful travel with fewer tourists.

  • Very clear skies and sharp mountain views
  • Cold mornings, especially in Nagarkot and Pokhara
  • Perfect for a relaxed Old Age Tour in Nepal and cultural exploration
  • Less crowded UNESCO heritage sites

Great for budget travelers and quiet Sightseeing in Nepal

Monsoon Season (June to August) – Green but Rainy

Monsoon brings heavy rain but also lush landscapes.

  • Green hills and a fresh environment
  • Occasional blocked mountain views due to clouds
  • Travel delays possible in hilly regions
  • Suitable for off-season travelers and cultural tours

Not ideal for sunrise views, but good for cultural Kathmandu Valley Sightseeing

Kathmandu Pokhara Sunrise Tour Itinerary: Day by Day

6 Days program for Kathmandu Pokhara Sunrise Tour. Daily timings and sequences remain flexible according to weather, flight operations, and group pace.

Day 1: Arrival in Kathmandu 1345 m, Transfer to Hotel

Approx. 1,345 m / 4,413 ft

Accommodation: 3-star Categories Hotel

Arrival at Tribhuvan International Airport. You will be greeted and warmly welcomed by Nepal Unique Treks. Drive 6.5 km to reach your Hotel in Thamel. After checking the Hotel, our expert will brief you about the tour and complete the official agreement for the Kathmandu Pokhara Sunrise Tour. Overnight Stay at Hotel.

Day 2: Patan, Bhaktapur sightseeing and drive to Nagarkot 2195 m, 6 hrs

Approx. 2,195 m / 7,201 ft

Duration: 6 hrs
Accommodation: Hotel Country Villa
Meals: Breakfast, Lunch and Dinner

After having breakfast you will be introduced to our city tour guide who will explain Nepal's heritage, culture, religion, people's lifestyle, and more about Nepal. It will depend on your interest if you want to know more about Nepal. Our guide will be eligible to tell stories and histories. Kathmandu Pokhara Sunrise Tour starts from Kathmandu with a private tour guide and private tourist vehicle. Your guide will bring you to explore world heritage sites in Bhaktapur city through Patan Durbar Squire. Patan Durbar Squire also known as the (Kalighad) city of Artisans in Lalitpur. There was a home of the Malla kings in ancient times. Now Patan Has was restored as a museum which are popular people known as Patan Museum and lots of domestic, and international tourists explore there. Patan is located in the southern part of the valley, and its shelf we known as Newari town with its rich Culture and tradition. You will converse with local culture through your tour guide to know, and understand many more about Patan ancient practices. After visiting Patan your guide will bring you to the World Heritage sites of Bhaktapur it takes 30 m from Patan Durbar Squire. We know Bhaktapur is a city of god because there are many religious people community from ancient times who believe in god and every morning they worship in the temple and they make wishes for a good day. We will visit the heritage sites of Bhaktapur Durbar Squire which display a clear picture of ancient medieval arts, local culture, and lifestyle of the Newari People. Also, you have a chance to test Newari food as lunch or, of your choice, we have many types of foreign restaurants. Drive from Bhaktapur to Nagarkot, check in Hotel, and after refreshing explore the sunrise view from where we can see Mt Everest Himalayan range, Langtang Himal, Annapurna, Manaslu, and the beautiful valley of Kavre village. Nagarkot is located in the southeastern part of Kathmandu. Which is 32 km far and it takes 1 hour 30 m by car. Enjoy your dinner in a beautiful and clean atmosphere. Early in the morning, you will see the beauty of Nagarkot with a gorgeous sunrise view and the magnificent Himalayan range, and you will know the reason to visit Nagarkot. After breakfast straight drive back to Kathmandu to explore the (Hindu Temple) Pashupatinath, Bouddhanath Stupa, and Swayambhunath (Monkey Temple). In the evening back to the Hotel. Overnight stay at Kathmandu.

Day 3: Flight from Kathmandu to Pokhara and Sightseeing 810 m

Approx. 810 m / 2,657 ft

Accommodation: 3-star Categories Hotel
Meals: Breakfast

Today we will visit another tourist city from where we can explore the massive Annapurna Himalaya range. After breakfast drives to Kathmandu domestic airport and takes a flight to Pokhara. From the airport, 5 m drive to your Hotel. Your guide will tell you about today's activities. After lunch, you can visit the shrine of goddess Taal Barahi Temple across Phewa Lake by boat. This Temple is particularly famous for local people. They make wishes for success in love and new families pray to the shrine of goddess Durga, and enjoy their day by boating in the Lake. You can visit the International Mountain Museum where you can learn about all Nepali culture, religion, flora, fauna, Village people's lifestyle, and geography. David waterfalls, Gupteswar Mahadev cave, and World Peace Stupa. Back to the lakeside and enjoy your dinner. Overnight stay in Pokhara.

Day 4: Visit Sarangkot Viewpoint (1,600m/5,249ft) Explore Pokhara 810 m, 6 hrs

Approx. 810 m / 2,657 ft

Duration: 6 hrs
Accommodation: 3-star Categories Hotel
Meals: Breakfast

Sarangkot Viewpoint is our major destination for our Kathmandu Pokhara Sunrise Tour. And it's located on the western side of Pokhara. Early in the morning, 40 m drive to the viewpoint. From there you will be mesmerized by the panoramic mountain views. Dramatically sun rays hit the top of Annapurna, Dhaulagiri, Machhapuchere, and other mountains. This is the best viewpoint across Nepal. Morning first sun rays of the sun change the golden mountains are a feast for your eyes. After breakfast, you will do adventure activities like Paragliding, Bungee Jump, Jeep flyer, mountain biking, and other tourist activities. In the evening stroll around Fewa Lake and Lakeside. Overnight stay at Pokhara.

Day 5: Drive from Pokhara to Kathmandu 7/8 hrs, 1345 m

Approx. 1,345 m / 4,413 ft

Duration: 7/8 hrs
Accommodation: 3-star Categories Hotel
Meals: Breakfast, Lunch and Dinner

Early in the morning your guide will bring you to the bus station and drive back to Kathmandu by luxury tourist bus it takes 7/8 hrs depending on the highway traffic situation. You will have a new experience of taking a bus drive in the mountains somehow you will enjoy with paradise view of villages, white water rafting river, and agriculture fields. Early evening you will arrive in Kathmandu. At 6 pm, in the evening you will be invited by Nepal Unique Treks to join us for a farewell dinner. Sharing your travel experience in Nepal and you will receive a trip completion certificate as a gift. Overnight stay at Kathmandu.

Day 6: Departure from Kathmandu 1345 m

Approx. 1,345 m / 4,413 ft

Accommodation: 3-star Hotel
Meals: Breakfast

Your Kathmandu Pokhara Sunrise tour finally comes to an end on this day. After breakfast, pack up your belongings, and Nepal Unique Treks staff will transfer to the Tribhuvan International Airport for a final departure.
