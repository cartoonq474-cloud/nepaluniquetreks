Chitwan National Park Jungle Safari & Tharu Cultural Tour

Discover Nepal's rich wildlife in Chitwan National Park with deep jungle jeep safaris, river canoeing, and Tharu tribal traditions. Chitwan is a UNESCO World Heritage natural sanctuary home to Asian one-horned rhinos, Royal Bengal tigers, mugger crocodiles, and over 500 species of exotic birds.

Key Tour Facts

Duration 3 Days / 2 Nights
Tour Difficulty Easy / Wildlife
Max Altitude 415 m
Destination Nepal

Tour Overview

Discover Nepal's rich wildlife in Chitwan National Park with deep jungle jeep safaris, river canoeing, and Tharu tribal traditions.

Tour Highlights

✓
Jeep Jungle Safari Deep forest safari spotting rhinos, wild elephants, deer, and leopards.
✓
Rapti River Canoe Ride Traditional dugout canoe ride observing marsh muggers and gharial crocodiles.
✓
Tharu Cultural Dance Vibrant evening stick dance and music performance by indigenous Tharu villagers.

Day-by-Day Detailed Itinerary

Day 1: Drive to Chitwan National Park & Tharu Village Walk

Kathmandu to Chitwan – 5 hours drive

Transport / Activity: Private AC Tourist Vehicle
Hotel: Jungle Safari Resort in Chitwan
Meals: Lunch & Dinner (L, D)
Overnight: Chitwan

Drive to Sauraha, Chitwan. Sunset view over Rapti River followed by Tharu cultural stick dance performance.

Day 2: Deep Jungle Jeep Safari, Canoe Ride & Elephant Center

Full Day Safari Activities

Transport / Activity: 4WD Open Safari Jeep & Canoe
Hotel: Jungle Safari Resort in Chitwan
Meals: Breakfast, Lunch & Dinner (B, L, D)
Overnight: Chitwan

Dugout canoe ride along Rapti river, guided nature walk, deep jeep safari inside park, and elephant breeding center visit.

Day 3: Bird Watching & Departure

Chitwan to Kathmandu / Pokhara

Transport / Activity: Private AC Tourist Vehicle
Hotel: Check-out
Meals: Breakfast (B)
Overnight: Departure

Early morning bird watching tour with resident naturalist. Breakfast before departure to Kathmandu or Pokhara.
