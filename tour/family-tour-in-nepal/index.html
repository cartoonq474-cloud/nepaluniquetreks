Kathmandu Pokhara and Chitwan Tour

This is an ideal tour package for family tours with children. This easy trekking starts from Kathmandu where you can explore the unique traditions and culture of the local peoples and additionally explore the very beautiful Temples that have been enlisted as world heritage sites by UNESCO, also the famous Buddhist stupa like Boudhanath and Changunarayan.

Pokhara is the city of lakes where you will be rewarded with astounding mountain panoramas and the chance to explore the big lakes in a tranquil environment. The view of the surrounding is also perfect in Pokhara. Then you will make a visit to CNP (Chitwan National Park) where you will get a chance to explore rare and endangered wild animals and beautiful bird species. Also gets a chance to explore the national park riding in the back of an elephant. Also, jeep safari and kayaking are famous in Chitwan National Park which is suitable for a tour with family also.
