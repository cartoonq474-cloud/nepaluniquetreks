WHEN TO TREK

Nepal Trekking Seasons Guide

Choose the optimal time of year for your Himalayan expedition.

Prime Season

Spring

March — May

Mild temperatures, blooming rhododendron forests, and clear morning vistas. High passes are open and Everest climbing expeditions are active at base camp.

Peak Popularity

Autumn

September — November

The gold standard for Himalayan trekking. Post-monsoon air provides crystal-clear mountain views, stable high-pressure weather, and festive Dashain/Tihar culture.

Crisp & Quiet

Winter

December — February

Crisp azure skies, quiet uncrowded trails, and snow-capped panoramas. Ideal for lower-altitude routes like Poon Hill, Mardi Himal, Helambu, and Pikey Peak.

Rain-Shadow

Summer / Monsoon

June — August

While most valleys receive monsoon rain, trans-Himalayan rain-shadow regions like Upper Mustang and Upper Dolpo remain dry, sunny, and exceptionally vibrant.

For first-time trekkers or travelers seeking gentle mountain adventures, Ghorepani Poon Hill (5 Days), Mardi Himal Ridge Trek (6 Days), and Langtang Valley Trek (8 Days) are ideal. They offer stunning mountain vistas, comfortable teahouses, well-graded stone trails, and safe maximum elevations below 4,500m.

The two primary trekking seasons in Nepal are Autumn (September to November) with crystal clear skies and stable temperatures, and Spring (March to May) known for blooming rhododendron forests and warm hiking weather. Trans-Himalayan rain-shadow regions like Upper Mustang and Dolpo are also fantastic during summer/monsoon (June to August).
