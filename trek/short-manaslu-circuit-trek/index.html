Short Manaslu Circuit Trek Overview

The Short Manaslu Circuit Trek is a remarkable journey through one of Nepal’s most pristine and less crowded trekking regions. Designed for trekkers with limited time, this 10 day Manaslu Circuit Trek itinerary captures the essence of the full circuit while ensuring comfort and proper acclimatization. The route follows the spectacular Mount Manaslu trekking route, leading through lush forests, river gorges, and high alpine landscapes.

As a restricted-area trekking experience, the Manaslu Circuit Trek requires special permits to preserve culture and limit visitor numbers compared to popular routes. This makes it a perfect option for those looking for the Manaslu Circuit Trek without crowds. Along the trail, trekkers encounter charming Tibetan-influenced villages, such as Manaslu, offering a unique cultural trekking experience in Nepal.

One of the highlights is crossing the breathtaking Larkya La Pass trek, one of the most scenic high passes in the Himalayas. Despite being a short itinerary, the trek is moderately challenging, making it important to understand the Manaslu Circuit Trek difficulty level before planning. With proper guidance, it is accessible to fit and determined trekkers.

The best time for the Manaslu Circuit Trek is during spring and autumn when the weather is stable, and the mountain views are clear. Many travelers choose a guided Manaslu Circuit Trek Nepal cost package, which includes experienced guides and porter services for safety and convenience.

This trek also stands out among short trekking routes in Nepal and is often compared in discussions like Manaslu Circuit Trek vs Annapurna Base Camp Trek. For those seeking an affordable Manaslu Circuit Trek package or exploring Nepal trekking packages 2026, this journey offers exceptional value, combining remote wilderness, rich culture, and unforgettable Himalayan scenery.

Why Choose Manaslu Circuit Trek?

The Manaslu Circuit Trek in Nepal stands out for its raw beauty and cultural authenticity. As a restricted area trekking in Nepal, it preserves its pristine environment and traditional lifestyle. This makes it an ideal choice for travelers seeking a more peaceful and exclusive trekking experience.

Unlike more commercial routes, the Manaslu region offers a true wilderness adventure combined with comfortable tea house trekking. It is also a great alternative often discussed in comparisons like Manaslu Circuit Trek vs Annapurna Circuit, especially for those wanting a less crowded trail.

With Nepal Unique Treks, you get a professionally guided experience, including expert guides and porter services, ensuring safety and comfort throughout your journey.

Difficulty Level

The Manaslu Circuit Trek difficulty is considered moderate to challenging. Although the itinerary is shortened, trekkers still need good physical fitness and stamina. Daily walking ranges from 5 to 7 hours, with gradual altitude gain.

The most demanding section is crossing Larkya La Pass (5,160m), where altitude and weather conditions can be challenging. However, with proper acclimatization, a well-planned itinerary, and guidance from experienced professionals, the trek is achievable for most fit trekkers.

Best Time to Do Manaslu Circuit Trek

The best time for the Manaslu Circuit Trek is during:

  • Spring (March to May): Clear skies, blooming rhododendrons, and pleasant temperatures
  • Autumn (September to November): Stable weather, excellent visibility, and ideal trekking conditions

Winter is colder with heavy snow at higher passes, while the monsoon season brings rain and landslides, making trekking more difficult.

Short Manaslu Circuit Trek Itinerary: Day by Day

10 Days program for Short Manaslu Circuit Trek. Daily timings and sequences remain flexible according to weather, flight operations, and group pace.

Day 1: Drive from Kathmandu to Machha Khola, Distance 160 km (drive), Altitude 930 m, Duration 8–9 hours

Approx. 930 m / 3,051 ft

Duration: 8–9 hours
Distance: 160 km
Accommodation: Guesthouse
Meals: Breakfast, Lunch and Dinner

We start our Manaslu Circuit Trek with a drive from Kathmandu to Machha Khola. The 160 km journey takes around 8-9 hours. We pass through winding mountain roads alongside the Trishuli and Budhi Gandaki rivers. The views of terraced hills, waterfalls, and rural Nepalese life are amazing. We go through Arughat and Soti Khola. See the difference between urban and remote Himalayan villages. This drive helps us understand culture and geography. Although it’s long, the journey is rewarding with changing scenery and traditional Gurung settlements. When we arrive in Machha Khola, we can rest at a teahouse. Get ready for the trek.

Day 2: Trek from Machha Khola to Jagat, Distance 22 km, Altitude 1,340 m, Duration 6-7 hours

Approx. 1,340 m / 4,396 ft

Duration: 6-7 hours
Distance: 22 km
Accommodation: Guesthouse
Meals: Breakfast, Lunch and Dinner

Today, we start our trek. We follow the Budhi Gandaki River through forests and narrow trails. We cross suspension bridges. Pass small settlements like Khorlabesi and Tatopani. Tatopani has hot springs. The trail has ascents and descents, making it a moderate start to the Manaslu Circuit Trek. As we approach Jagat, the landscape gets rugged. We see stone-paved paths. Jagat is a village and the entry to the restricted Manaslu region. We need permits to enter. The area has a Tibetan-influenced culture with prayer flags and mani walls. This day is perfect for trekkers who want a Himalayan experience.

Day 3: Trek from Jagat to Deng, Distance 20 km, Altitude 1,860 m, Duration: 6-7 hours

Approx. 1,860 m / 6,102 ft

Duration: 6-7 hours
Distance: 20 km
Accommodation: Guesthouse
Meals: Breakfast, Lunch and Dinner

Today we trek deeper into the Manaslu Conservation Area. The landscapes are dramatic. The elevation increases. We pass through forests, waterfalls, and traditional villages like Philim. Philim is known for its Gurung culture. We cross suspension bridges. See narrow gorges carved by the Budhi Gandaki River. The route offers views of cliffs and waterfalls. As we move higher, the vegetation changes, and Tibetan culture becomes more noticeable. Deng is a village with forests and a peaceful overnight stay. This section highlights the region's biodiversity and cultural richness.

Day 4: Trek from Deng to Namrung, Distance 19 km, Altitude 2,630 m, Duration 6-7 hours

Approx. 2,630 m / 8,629 ft

Duration: 6-7 hours
Distance: 19 km
Accommodation: Guesthouse
Meals: Breakfast, Lunch and Dinner

The trail winds through pine and rhododendron forests as it gains altitude. We pass villages like Bihi and Ghap. We see stones, chortens, and Buddhist prayer flags. These show the Tibetan influence in the region. The path gets quieter and more remote. We feel like we're on a remote Himalayan trekking adventure. As we approach Namrung, we see snow-capped peaks, including Manaslu. Namrung is a developed village with teahouses and stunning mountain views. It's a place to experience local culture and acclimatize.

Day 5: Trek from Namrung to Samagaon, Distance 17 km, Altitude 3,530 m, Duration: 6-7 hours

Approx. 3,530 m / 11,581 ft

Duration: 6-7 hours
Distance: 17 km
Accommodation: Guesthouse
Meals: Breakfast, Lunch and Dinner

Today, we trek through one of the scenic sections of the Short Manaslu Circuit Trek. We pass through Lho village. See magnificent views of Mount Manaslu. We go through Shyala village surrounded by peaks. The landscape becomes more alpine. We see views of Manaslu, Himalchuli, and other peaks. It's a photographer's paradise. Samagaon is a Tibetan-influenced village with monasteries and traditional houses. It's an acclimatization stop before crossing high passes.

Day 6: Trek from Samagaon to Samdo, Distance 8 km, Altitude 3,875 m, Duration 3-4 hours

Approx. 3,875 m / 12,713 ft

Duration: 3-4 hours
Distance: 8 km
Accommodation: Guesthouse
Meals: Breakfast, Lunch and Dinner

Today is a day for trekking to properly acclimatize. We gently ascend through landscapes with stunning mountain views. We pass yak pastures. See close-up views of Manaslu and the surrounding peaks. Samdo is a Tibetan refugee village near the Nepal-Tibet border. It offers a cultural experience. Trekkers often explore hills for acclimatization and panoramic views. This day is crucial for adjusting to altitudes before crossing Larkya La Pass.

Day 7: Trek from Samdo to Dharamsala (Larkya Phedi), Distance 7 km, Altitude 4,460 m, Duration 4-5 hours

Approx. 4,460 m / 14,633 ft

Duration: 4-5 hours
Distance: 7 km
Accommodation: Guesthouse
Meals: Breakfast, Lunch and Dinner

We trek to Dharamsala, also known as Larkya Phedi. It's the base for crossing Larkya La Pass. The trail is gradual. Gets more challenging due to the higher altitude. We walk through terrain with minimal vegetation. The views are spectacular with glaciers and snow-covered mountains. Dharamsala is a stop with limited facilities. It's essential for acclimatization and preparation for the days passing crossing.

Day 8: Cross Larkya La Pass and Trek to Bimthang, Distance 18 km, and Altitude 5,106 m (pass) 3,590 m (Bimthang), Duration 8-9 hours

Approx. 5,016 m / 16,457 ft

Duration: 8-9 hours
Distance: 18 km
Accommodation: Guesthouse
Meals: Breakfast, Lunch and Dinner

This is the challenging and rewarding day of the Short Manaslu Circuit Trek. We ascend to Larkya La Pass, one of the trekking passes in Nepal. The climb is steep. Offers breathtaking views. We see Himlung, Cheo Himal, Kang Guru, and Annapurna II. After reaching the pass, we descend to Bimthang through valleys and moraines. The dramatic change in the landscape makes this day unforgettable. Bimthang is a valley surrounded by mountains and forests.

Day 9: Trek from Bimthang to Dharapani, Distance 24 km, Altitude 1,960 m, Duration 7-8 hours

Approx. 1,960 m / 6,430 ft

Duration: 7-8 hours
Distance: 24 km
Accommodation: Guesthouse
Meals: Breakfast, Lunch and Dinner

We descend from Bimthang through rhododendron forests, meadows, and traditional villages. The landscape becomes greener as we lose altitude. We pass villages like Tilije. Experience the transition into the Annapurna region. The trail is relatively easier with descents and scenic views. Dharapani is a village with better facilities. It marks the end of the trekking section.

Day 10: Drive from Dharapani to Kathmandu, Distance 200 km, Altitude 1,400 m (Kathmandu), Duration 8-9 hours

Approx. 1,400 m / 4,593 ft

Duration: 8-9 hours
Distance: 200 km
Meals: Breakfast, Lunch and Dinner

The day involves a scenic drive back to Kathmandu via Besisahar. The journey offers a look at the Himalayan landscapes and rural villages. We reflect on the adventure of the Short Manaslu Circuit Trek in Nepal. This trek is perfect for those seeking a yet rewarding Himalayan experience. It combines mountain views, rich culture, and challenging terrain. Upon arrival in Kathmandu, we can celebrate the completion of our trek.

Frequently Asked Questions for Short Manaslu Circuit Trek

What is the Short Manaslu Circuit Trek?

∨
The Short Manaslu Circuit Trek is a condensed version of the classic Manaslu Circuit, designed for trekkers with limited time. It typically takes 10–12 days and covers the major highlights, including traditional villages, Buddhist culture, and the stunning Larkya La Pass (5,106m). This trek is perfect for those seeking an off-the-beaten-path trekking experience in Nepal with fewer crowds compared to the Everest Base Camp Trek and the Annapurna Base Camp Trek.

How difficult is the Short Manaslu Circuit Trek?

∨
The Short Manaslu Circuit Trek difficulty level is considered moderate to challenging. Trekkers walk 6–8 hours daily on varied terrain, including steep ascents and high-altitude passes. Crossing Larkya La Pass is the most demanding part. A good level of fitness and prior trekking experience are recommended for a safe and enjoyable Himalayan trekking adventure in Nepal.

How many days are needed for the Short Manaslu Circuit Trek?

∨
The Short Manaslu Circuit Trek itinerary usually takes 10 to 12 days, depending on acclimatization and transportation choices. This shorter version still includes all major highlights, making it ideal for trekkers who want a complete Manaslu trekking experience in a limited timeframe.

What is the best time for the Short Manaslu Circuit Trek?

∨
The best time for the Short Manaslu Circuit Trek in Nepal is during spring (March–May) and autumn (September–November). These seasons offer clear skies, stable weather, and excellent mountain views. Trekking during these months ensures the best experience of the Manaslu region trekking route

Do I need a permit for the Short Manaslu Circuit Trek?

∨
Yes, permits are mandatory for the Manaslu Circuit Trek. Trekkers need a Restricted Area Permit (RAP), Manaslu Conservation Area Permit (MCAP), and Annapurna Conservation Area Permit (ACAP). Hiring a licensed guide is also compulsory, making this trek more organized and safer for travelers.

Is a guide required for the Manaslu Circuit Trek?

∨
Yes, hiring a licensed guide is compulsory for the Short Manaslu Circuit Trek due to its restricted area status. Trekking must be done in a group of at least two people with a registered agency. A guide enhances safety and provides valuable cultural and geographical insights during the Manaslu trekking experience.

What is the highest point of the Short Manaslu Circuit Trek?

∨
The highest point of the Short Manaslu Circuit Trek is Larkya La Pass at 5,106 meters. It is one of the highest trekking passes in Nepal and offers breathtaking panoramic views of Himalayan peaks, making it a major highlight of the Manaslu trekking route.

Is altitude sickness a concern on this trek?

∨
Yes, altitude sickness can be a concern during the Short Manaslu Circuit Trek, especially above 3,000 meters. Proper acclimatization, hydration, and a gradual ascent are essential. Including acclimatization days in your Manaslu Circuit itinerary helps reduce the risk significantly.

What kind of accommodation is available on the trek?

∨
Accommodation during the Short Manaslu Circuit Trek is mainly teahouses or lodges. These offer basic rooms, local meals, and a chance to interact with locals. While facilities are simpler than in popular regions, they provide an authentic Nepal trekking experience in the Himalayas.

How much does the Short Manaslu Circuit Trek cost?

∨
The cost of the Short Manaslu Circuit Trek typically ranges from $900 to $1,500 per person, depending on group size, services, permits, guide, and transportation. This makes it a cost-effective option for those seeking a remote Himalayan trekking adventure in Nepal.

Is the Manaslu Circuit Trek less crowded?

∨
Yes, the Short Manaslu Circuit Trek is much less crowded compared to the Everest Base Camp and Annapurna Circuit. It is ideal for trekkers seeking a peaceful, off-the-beaten-path trekking experience in Nepal.

What should I pack for the Short Manaslu Circuit Trek?

∨
Essential packing items for the Short Manaslu Circuit Trek include warm clothing, trekking boots, a sleeping bag, a waterproof jacket, water purification tablets, and basic medical supplies. Proper gear ensures comfort and safety during your Manaslu trekking adventure.

Can beginners do the Short Manaslu Circuit Trek?

∨
Beginners can attempt the Short Manaslu Circuit Trek, but they should have good physical fitness and preparation. Training, proper acclimatization, and guidance from experienced trekking professionals are essential for a successful Manaslu trekking experience in Nepal.

How do I get to the starting point of the trek?

∨
The trek usually starts with a drive from Kathmandu to Machha Khola or Soti Khola. The journey takes 7–9 hours by jeep or bus. This scenic drive is part of the overall Manaslu Circuit Trek itinerary.

Why choose the Short Manaslu Circuit Trek?

∨
The Short Manaslu Circuit Trek offers a perfect mix of adventure, culture, and natural beauty in a shorter duration. With fewer crowds, stunning mountain views, and authentic Himalayan villages, it is one of the best options for an alternative trekking route in Nepal.
