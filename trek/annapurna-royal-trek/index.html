Annapurna Royal Trek Overview

The Annapurna Royal Trek was named after the royal family, Prince Charles did the trek. easy hike with a tremendous panorama Himalayas. Easy Trek Itinerary and cost for 2026 and 2027 by Nepal Unique Treks. Annapurna Royal Trek is a trekking route that stretches from Kalikasthan to Syaklung. It became the "Royal Trek" after Prince Charles from England and his entourage of 90 men walked on it in the 1980s. Since then, many trekkers, both Nepali and from foreign countries, have ascended this trail. Being a relatively easy and short trail leading to the Annapurna region, many beginner trekkers love this route.

Although the Royal Trek in the Annapurna region is short, it has so much to offer trekkers and nature lovers. From watching sky-towering Himalayas, serene lakes, rivers, and waterfalls to delving deep into the Gurung and Tamang culture, tradition, and lifestyle, this trek has so much. Moreover, you can also experience one of the best driving experiences from Kathmandu to Pokhara and then to Kalikasthan. From Kalikasthan, we begin the walking journey on the rest of the route.

Just as you begin the trek from Kalikasthan, you can find yourself surrounded by green hills, lush terraced fields, and panoramic Himalayan vistas, with the majestic Annapurna and Machhapuchhre (Fishtail) peaks rising in the distance. Every day, the trail reveals new scenes, scenarios, and charming Gurung and Magar settlements, where you can also see traditional farming communities, fragrant rhododendron forests, and awe-inspiring sunrises. Besides, the peaceful ridgeline walk from Syaklung to Chisopani to the magical descent to the Begnas Lake, the Annapurna Royal Trek offers the perfect combination of nature, culture, and comfort.

So, are you ready to go for one of the easy, short, and, of course, a royal trek in the Annapurna region? Check out the entire package before booking the package.

Annapurna Royal Trek Permits

If you want to go for the Annapurna Royal Trek, the trekkers must obtain two different permits, and they are:

  • Annapurna Conservation Area Permit (ACAP)

  • Trekkers' Information Management System (TIMS) Card

Both ACAP and TIMS can be obtained either in Kathmandu or Pokhara from the Tourism Board Office. Moreover, we also recommend that the trekkers not worry about obtaining these permits because the local representatives from Nepal Unique Treks & Expeditions are there to assist you.

8 Days program for Annapurna Royal Trek - Cost and Itinerary. Daily timings and sequences remain flexible according to weather, flight operations, and group pace.

Day 1: Arrival in Kathmandu - 1345 m.

Approx. 1,345 m / 4,413 ft

Accommodation: 3-star Hotel

Welcome to Nepal, the country of the Himalayas! As you land in the Kathmandu International Airport, our local representatives welcome you with a khada and drive you to the hotel in a private vehicle. Get ready to be welcomed again with a refreshing welcome drink once you reach the hotel. After that, you can head to your room and get some rest. For the rest of the day, we recommend that the trekkers explore the bustling streets of Thamel. This amazing street offers everything, including unique culture, tradition, and delicious cuisine. In the evening, we invite you for a traditional Nepali dinner as a welcoming gesture from Nepal Unique Treks & Expeditions. Just wait till we start the adventurous Royal Trek in Annapurna from the next day.

Day 2: Kathmandu to Pokhara - (810 m) 7 hrs

Approx. 810 m / 2,657 ft

Duration: 7 hrs
Accommodation: 3-star Hotel
Meals: Breakfast

Today, we lose some attitude as the local guides drive you from Kathmandu to Pokhara on a tourist bus. Well, this will be your transfer from the city of temples to the city of lakes. Yes, Pokhara is famous for its hustling, bustling, and amazing lakeside lifestyle. It takes about 7 to 8 hours for the tourist bus to reach Pokhara from Kathmandu, as we need to cover a total distance of 201 kilometers. The tourist bus directly takes you to the designated hotel, where once again, they welcome you with a refreshing welcome drink. As you have already reached Pokhara, why not spend the rest of the day roaming around the lakeside? Let us tell you that Pokhara's lakeside is one of the most well-decorated cities in Nepal. From peaceful lakeside spaces, a temple in the middle of the lake, to some lake-view hotels and cafes, you can find so much here. Therefore, we recommend that you spend some quality time in the aesthetic hotels and cafes or go for a quick ride to the nearby areas. The lakeside stays hustling and bustling until midnight. However, we recommend the trekkers to sleep a little early so that they can prepare for the journey that begins the next day.

Day 3: Drive from Pokhara to Bijayapur Khola and Trek to Kalikasthan - (1370 m) 5 hrs

Approx. 1,370 m / 4,495 ft

Duration: 5 hrs
Accommodation: Teahouse
Meals: Breakfast, Lunch and Dinner

On the third day, we divide the journey into two parts: first, a drive from Pokhara to Bijayapur Khola, and then a trek to Kalikasthan. The drive from Pokhara to Bijayaour, despite being just a 40-minute drive, takes us through one of the scenic routes. On your way to Bijaypur via tourist bus, you can watch the verdant fields, traditional villages, and views of the Himalayan foothills while we go past small farms, terraced farms. Moreover, you will also see children going to school or playing on their way. Just wave at them and see them smile the brightest while waving back at you. As we reach Bijaypur Khola or the Bijaypur Army Camp, it's time to begin the walking journey on the Annapurna Royal Trekking trail. The route is not very difficult as we only have to walk for about 5 hours. However, this section of the trail unveils the scenic hills, charming villages, and the true beauty of the Annapurna region. Walking past the Gurung and Brahmin villages, over the terraced farms, and walking the path through sal and chir pine forests, we make our way to Kalikasthan. In Kalikasthan, the trekkers can visit the Kalika temple while enjoying outstanding scenery and a sweeping view of the Begnas lake with mountains and the terraced hills. Spend the rest of the day and evening experiencing the crisp mountain air before having dinner and sleeping for the night.

Day 4: Trek from Kalikasthan to Syaklung - (1760 m) 6 hrs

Approx. 1,760 m / 5,774 ft

Duration: 6 hrs
Accommodation: Teahouse
Meals: Breakfast, Lunch and Dinner

Another day to walk on the scenic trail across the dense forests, rocky ridges, and remote communities. This section of the Annapurna Royal Trek requires us to walk for about 6 hours while covering steep ascents and descents. On your way to Syaklung, you will also be offered some of the stunning views of Annapurna II, Lamjung Himal, and Machhapuchhre (Fishtail Mountain). Besides, we also walk on a path that gently goes through serene meadows before reaching Syaklung. This village stands at an altitude of 1760 meters, offering a peaceful space in the hilltop with exceptionally warm hospitality and a serene environment.

Day 5: Descend from Syaklung to Chisopani - (1550 m) 6 hrs

Approx. 1,550 m / 5,085 ft

Duration: 6 hrs
Accommodation: Teahouse
Meals: Breakfast, Lunch and Dinner

Well, Syaklung stands as the highest point of the entire Annapurna Royal Trekking map. Therefore, we begin descending from the fifth day. The jaw-dropping scenes, dense forests, and mighty Himalayas still surround you to encourage your journey. Moreover, this section of the Royal Annapurna Trek also takes you across several ascents and descents before we finally reach Chisopani. Well, don't misunderstand Chisopani with Chisapani, which sits near Kathmandu. As you approach this beautifully settled village, the locals welcome you with a bright smile on their faces, and you can hear everyone saying "Namaste" while joining their hands. Get to the hotel, store your belongings, and then you can explore the village for the rest of the time. Let us tell you that watching the sunset from Chisopani stands as one of the best things that you can do in the entire trek. So, don't miss that at all. In the evening, you can also see the children returning to the playgrounds after completing their school. Besides, just sit in a windful environment watching the sunset and eye-soothing views of the Dhaulagiri and Annapurna ranges. After that, have your dinner in the hotel and go for a good rest.

Day 6: Trek to Begnas Lake,

Approx. 810 m / 2,657 ft

Accommodation: 3-star Hotel
Meals: Breakfast, Lunch

Wake up, get refreshed, and prepare yourself for a descending walk with one of the most serene endings. Yes, on the sixth day, we trek from Chisopani to Begnas Lake. Begnas Lake stands as one of the most beautiful and the second biggest lakes in Pokhara. A big lake with a stone-paved surface for you to walk on, a space where you can capture as many photos and videos as possible, while watching the surrounding hills, is what the Begnas Lake area presents. Moreover, you can also see a lot of fishermen with their fishing rods, sitting on the bank of the lake. Well, do you know that the Begnas Lake area is famous for an outstanding dish named "Majhikuna grilled fish?" Majhikuna, in Nepali, means fishermen's corner, which is exactly what Begnas Lake is. Talking about the route to Begnas Lake, this section of the trail takes us past tranquil woodlands and terraced farmland, while also letting trekkers witness the picturesque settlements of this area. Still out of the hustling and bustling areas of Pokhara, the trail sits completely relaxed and peaceful. Yes, enjoy the rest of the evening sightseeing the Begnas Lake and remember to try the delicious Majhikuna Grilled Fish.

Day 7: Drive back to Kathmandu

Approx. 1,345 m / 4,413 ft

Accommodation: 3-star Hotel
Meals: Breakfast

We are finally on the seventh day of the adventurous Annapurna Royal Trekking package, and it's time for us to return to Kathmandu. As you wake up and get ready, our representatives take you on a drive to Kathmandu, which takes about 6 to 7 hours. As we reach Kathmandu, our representatives will drive you to the hotel, where you can rest after such an amazing and tiring walk.

Traveller Useful Information

The Annapurna Royal Trek is considered to be a moderate to easy trekking route in the Himalayas of Nepal. If you have basic physical fitness, go for some basic exercises or practices; this trekking route stands as the best adventurous option for you. Moreover, the Annapurna Royal Trek also stands as the best option for anyone seeking a relaxed cultural and scenic experience without venturing into high altitudes.

Talking about the entire Annapurna Royal Trekking trail, you can expect the entire route to be well-trodden and not overly steep or rugged. In most of the sections. The trekkers have to walk through gentle hills, forests, terraced fields, and traditional Gurung villages. The best thing about this trekking route is that the highest point of this trek stands at an altitude of 1,760 meters above sea level, and we only have to walk for about 4 to 6 hours per day.

Due to this trek being comparatively easy, you also do not need to be a seasoned trekker or an experienced mountaineer. However, remember to go for a basic fitness level and focus on your legs. Strong legs are very important for trekkers to walk for 4 to 6 hours every day. In a nutshell, the Annapurna Royal Trekking route is not a very difficult trekking route, and it only requires a basic fitness level and some basic preparation.

Whether you are going for a difficult trekking adventure or a comparatively easy one like the Annapurna Royal Trek, you must be aware of the season. Yes, the season, weather, and climatic conditions play important roles in making any trekking journey easy or difficult. So, if you want to go for an easy yet adventurous trek on the Annapurna Royal Trekking route, we recommend you choose the Spring season (March to May) or the Autumn season (September to November).

These two seasons stand as the best seasons for the Annapurna Royal Trek for the following reasons:

  • During the spring season, skies remain very clear, offering outstanding views of the Himalayas, valleys, and lakes.

  • The Monsoon season, being the Post-monsoon season, again offers the best visibility of the Annapurna range.

  • Most sections of the Annapurna Royal Trek remain dry, and the weather remains stable, offering a safe and more enjoyable trek.

  • In the spring season, you can expect pleasant daytime temperatures and blooming rhododendron forests.

  • Besides, the autumn season also stands as the perfect time for cultural and traditional experiences in the local villages, as they would have just completed harvesting the grains.

  • Overall, the entire trek will be full of amazing experiences, a cultural and traditional heaven during the spring and autumn seasons.

Besides, if you want the trek to be a little more challenging and difficult, we also recommend that you go for this trek at the start of the winter season (December). However, you must be very careful while trekking this season. That is because in the winter season, the route can be very cold, many hotels and lodges remain closed, and you might also not get good views due to foggy weather.

Throughout the Annapurna Royal Trek, the trekkers can expect a chance to enjoy traditional Nepali meals along with some basic international dishes as well. Moreover, the meals are also served in local tea houses or even in the cafes or restaurants of your choice. While you stay in Pokhara, you can explore a lot of cafes, restaurants, hotels, etc that offer almost every type of cuisine and delicacies. However, while you are walking on the trail, go for the Nepali Thali set because it not only fulfills your hunger but also provides much energy for you to stay active and healthy during the trek.

The Nepal Unique Treks & Expeditions team makes sure to arrange three meals every day throughout the trek. You can expect breads, coffee, tea, omelets, boiled eggs, etc, for breakfast and Nepali Thali, momo, chowmein, Thukpa, etc, for lunch and dinner, Moreover, the hotels and lodges that we arrange also provide the trekkers with seasonal fruits as dessert.

As the Annapurna Royal Trek is a moderate to easy trekking route, you will not have to go for hardcore training or preparation. However, we recommend the trekkers to go for at least basic training and preparation, which are listed below:

  • Trekkers must prioritize physical exercises like running, jogging, swimming, and especially doing activities that make their legs stronger.

  • We recommend that you study the culture, tradition, and lifestyle of the Annapurna Royal Trekking region.

  • Make sure that you obtain trekking permits and passes a few days before going for the trek.

  • We also recommend that the trekkers get travel insurance. For that, make sure that the insurance covers trekking up to 2,000 to 3,000 meters above sea level.
