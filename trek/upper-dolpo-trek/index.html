Upper Dolpo Trek is the enchanting traveling up in the Himalayas near the vicinity of the Tibet and Nepal border. The place contains the spectacular Himalayan ranges which include the mammoth Dhaulagiri Mountain. Trekking in Nepal at Dolpo region is termed as the strenuous as well as challenging which presents the unique wilderness adventure memories.

The main focus point of the Dhaulagiri Trek is the Shey Phoksundo Lake. Apart from this deepest lake of Nepal the Shey Gompa Monastery is another highlighting prospect of the region. Witness the plethora of the Himalayan animals and birds like blue sheep, yak, snow leopard, that and others in the region. The place is in the northern territory of Nepal. Therefore, the people rely upon the Tibetan Trade. The cultural and traditional beliefs, as well as the daily lifestyle, resembles that of the Tibetan counterparts. The major religion of the people residing in the Dolpo is Buddhism however in the remote parts they still practice Bonpo. The journey would be all the way following the Phoksundo River originated from the Lake itself.

Once you have reached the Shey Phoksundo you will be awestruck with the magnificent delights of the Mother Nature. The Himalayas smiling in the background and rocky terrain with beautiful landscape remind of the fairyland. The Lake is surrounded by the Himalayas and blessed by the diversified natural constituents. The experience of the subtropical to the alpine forests environment really makes the trip eventful. The journey passes by the Ringmo and further towards Palam crossing the cedar forest. The route comprises of the gigantic waterfalls and the rocky hills which would provide enough tranquility and wilderness adventure feeling. Observing such magnificence of nature the trekkers hardly get time to think about the fatigue. The Shey Phoksundo National Park visit is another element of the successful trip of the Upper Dolpo.

Book the Upper Dolpo Trek with the reliable team of the Nepal Unique Treks & Expedition. We will ensure you to have the great environment and impeccable hospitality while traveling to this off the beaten track.
