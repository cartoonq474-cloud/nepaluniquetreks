Overview of Annapurna Base Camp Short Trek

The Annapurna Base Camp Short Trek is one of the most rewarding short treks in Nepal, offering a perfect combination of natural beauty, cultural richness, and Himalayan adventure within a limited timeframe. Ideal for those who want to experience the grandeur of the Annapurna Himalayas but have a tight schedule, this trek leads you to the base of the world’s 10th-highest mountain, Annapurna I (8,091 m), in just 7 days.

At Nepal Unique Treks, we have carefully crafted this Annapurna Base Camp Trek itinerary to cater to adventurers who seek a fast yet immersive Himalayan experience. Also known as the ABC Short Trek, this journey starts from Pokhara and takes you through terraced farmlands, rhododendron forests, and traditional Gurung villages, all the way up to the majestic Annapurna Sanctuary.

The highlight of this trek is reaching Annapurna Base Camp Trek, surrounded by stunning peaks like Machapuchare (Fishtail), Hiunchuli, Annapurna South, and more. Our Short Annapurna Base Camp Trek plan ensures that trekkers can enjoy the full ABC experience without rushing or missing key attractions along the way.

This Short ABC Trek Nepal is designed for trekkers with moderate fitness levels. It offers excellent acclimatization, balanced walking hours, and enough time to explore the cultural and natural highlights of the region. It’s an ideal choice for solo travelers, couples, groups, and even families who want to enjoy a memorable Himalayan journey in a shorter period.

The Annapurna Short Trekking Package by Nepal Unique Treks includes everything you need for a smooth trekking experience: expert local guides, comfortable accommodations, hygienic meals, and personalized service. Whether you’re looking for a Short Annapurna Trek Itinerary, a quick adventure, or simply a well-organized mountain getaway, this trek delivers it all.

Often referred to as the Annapurna Base Camp Fast Trek, ABC Trek in Short Time, or Annapurna Base Camp Express Trek, this version of the classic ABC route is gaining popularity among time-bound travelers who still want the thrill of high-altitude trekking. You'll witness diverse landscapes, soak in natural hot springs, and enjoy awe-inspiring mountain vistas every step of the way.

A Short Annapurna Base Camp Hike like this doesn’t compromise on scenery or experience. Best Time to Do the Short Annapurna Base Camp Trek

Choosing the right season is crucial for a successful and enjoyable Short Annapurna Base Camp Trek. The Annapurna region offers different experiences throughout the year, but some seasons stand out as the best.

Spring Season (March to May) – Best Overall Time

Spring is widely considered the best time for the Annapurna Base Camp trek.

  • Clear skies with excellent mountain visibility
  • Blooming rhododendron forests across the trail
  • Moderate temperatures are ideal for trekking
  • Stunning views of Annapurna I, Machapuchare, and Hiunchuli

Perfect for: Nature lovers, photographers, and first-time trekkers


Autumn Season (September to November) Peak Trekking Season

Autumn is another top season for the short Annapurna Base Camp trek.

  • Stable weather with crystal-clear Himalayan views
  • Dry trails and comfortable trekking conditions
  • Major festivals like Dashain and Tihar enhance the cultural experience

Winter trekking is possible but comes with challenges.

  • Fewer crowds, peaceful trekking experience
  • Clear skies but colder temperatures, especially at higher altitudes
  • Snowfall may block some trails

Suitable for: Experienced trekkers seeking solitude

Difficulty Level of Short Annapurna Base Camp Trek

The Short Annapurna Base Camp Trek is considered moderate in difficulty, making it suitable for beginners with a good fitness level. This popular Annapurna Base Camp short trek involves daily hikes of 5–7 hours through varied terrain, including stone steps, rainforest trails, and gradual ascents. While no technical climbing is required, altitude (up to 4,130m at Annapurna Base Camp) can be challenging for some trekkers. Proper acclimatization, basic preparation, and a steady pace help ensure success. Overall, the ABC trek difficulty level is manageable, offering a rewarding Himalayan adventure for those seeking a short trekking experience in Nepal.

Annapurna Base Camp Short Trek Itinerary: Day by Day

7 Days program for Annapurna Base Camp Short Trek. Daily timings and sequences remain flexible according to weather, flight operations, and group pace.

Day 1: Nayapul, Syauli Bazar, Kimche, and Trek to Ghandruk village (1940m / 6,364 ft )

Approx. 1,932 m / 6,339 ft

Accommodation: Guesthouse
Meals: Breakfast, Lunch and Dinner

Our trek starts from Pokhara. However, Nepal Unique Treks provide a tourist vehicle to drive to Nayapul. The trekking goes through Birethati via Syauli Bazaar and the trek ends at Ghandruk Village. During the trekking, you will see incredible mountains, beautiful lush Himalayan, terraced farms, and subtropical forests. Ghandruk village people follow Buddha Dharma and there is a group of the Gurung community. They have a beautiful culture and local traditions and they have their language. Villages are decorated by community homestays and luxury guesthouses. You can enjoy the view of Mt Fishtail, Mt Annapurna South, and Himchuli. In the surrounding village, you can explore a school, Monastery, Temple, and Gurung Museum. In the evening you can see a dramatic mountain view with a sunset. Overnight sleep at Ghandruk Village.

Day 2: Trek start from Ghandruk to Chomrong (2170m / 7,119ft), 6 hrs

Approx. 2,170 m / 7,119 ft

Duration: 6 hrs
Accommodation: Guesthouse
Meals: Breakfast, Lunch and Dinner

Enjoy the morning by having tea or coffee with beautiful sunshine. After breakfast, trek from Ghandruk village through a steep downhill to Kimrong River and then a short ascent to arrive at Chhomrong Village. Today from Chhomrong also you will see a similar Mountain View that you saw already from Ghandruk but a closer view. Overnight sleep at Guesthouse.

Day 3: Trek from Chomrong to Himalaya (2,890m / 9,425ft), 6 hrs

Approx. 2,890 m / 9,482 ft

Duration: 6 hrs
Accommodation: Guesthouse
Meals: Breakfast, Lunch and Dinner

Early morning sunrays hit the top of Mt Annapurna and it looks so beautiful, and then after breakfast, our trek starts to the Himalayas where you spend a Night. Our trek passes through the lush Himalaya forest, crossing a suspension bridge then the path ascends to reach Sinuwa Village. Then today trek gradually uphill to Bamboo. If you are trekking during the spring session trekking trails are covered by the Nepali National flower Rhododendron all forest,

Day 4: Trek from Himalaya to Annapurna Base Camp (4,130m/13,550 ft), 6 hrs

Approx. 4,130 m / 13,550 ft

Duration: 6 hrs
Accommodation: Guesthouse
Meals: Breakfast, Lunch and Dinner

Trek starts from the Himalayas and follows trails that pass through the glacier of Modi Khola. Then trek ascends to Machhapuchre Base Camp. Trekking trails ultimately take you to the Annapurna Base Camp. Machhapuchre Base Camp allows you to kill your tiredness by having tea and coffee or refueling your body energy to summits The Annapurna Base Camp which is our major destination. Check-in at the guesthouse then early in the evening Base Camp offers you stunning Sunset and panoramic views of the surrounding mountains. We will walk closely by the Annapurna Base Camp Glaciers and we will pray for world-famous mountaineer climbers who lost life there during Annapurna Expeditions. and spend the night at Guesthouse. 4130 m from sea level.

Day 5: Trek from Annapurna Base Camp to Bamboo (2,350m / 7,700ft), 6/7 hrs

Approx. 2,350 m / 7,710 ft

Duration: 6/7 hrs
Accommodation: Guesthouse
Meals: Breakfast, Lunch and Dinner

Food Today you have the last opportunity to enjoy the thin air, Sunrise, panoramic Mountain View, glacier, and snow-capped mountain; you will have a fabulous morning that makes your lifetime memories. After enjoying breakfast we start our trek to Bamboo. That will eventually take us out of Annapurna Base Camp. However, we retrace to reach Bamboo Village. Overnight sleep at guesthouse.

Day 6: Trek from Bamboo to Jhinu Danda (1,780m / 6,393ft) Hotspring, 5 hrs

Approx. 1,777 m / 5,830 ft

Duration: 5 hrs
Accommodation: Guesthouse
Meals: Breakfast, Lunch and Dinner

Our trek continues down to Kimrong Khola, and from there, the trekking trail ascends and passes through Sinuwa village, rhododendron, bamboo, and oak forests, and after crossing the suspension bridge ultimately leading to Chomrong village. We will stop there for rest drink tea and coffee, and look back to the mountain to say goodbye. Almost 1 hrs descending to a peaceful settlement called Jhinu Danda. This village has hot springs, where we soothe our tired muscles. You will be staying at Jhinu Danda for the night. And we will celebrate with our staff for completing our destination and Happy Trek. Because tomorrow is the last day trek, when we reach Pokhara everyone will choose a different way to go back home.

Day 7: Trek from Jhinu Danda to Siwai and drive to Pokhara (820m ), 4 hrs

Approx. 810 m / 2,657 ft

Duration: 4 hrs
Accommodation: Lodge Tea House
Meals: Breakfast, Dinner

Today is the last day of our trek. After breakfast, we leave Jhinu Danda and cross the suspension bridge then follow the Mardi Khola with a local jeep drive we reach Pokhara via Ghandruk Phedi, Birethanti, and then Nayapul until we reach Pokhara. Our trekking package and official agreements will be completed at Pokhara. We wish by then, you have created precious time, and great memories of reaching Annapurna Base Camp 4130 m. At Pokhara, we will congratulate giving on the completion certificate of the Annapurna Base Camp Trek.

Frequently Asked Questions for Annapurna Base Camp Short Trek

How difficult is the Annapurna Base Camp Short Trek?

∨
The ABC Short Trek is moderately challenging, suitable for trekkers with average fitness levels. It involves 5–7 hours of walking per day on varied terrain, including stone steps, forest trails, and high-altitude sections. No technical climbing is required, but mental and physical preparation helps ensure a comfortable experience.

How many days does the short Annapurna Base Camp Trek take?

∨
This short itinerary typically takes 7 days, starting and ending in Pokhara. It offers a faster pace while still allowing you to reach the base of the stunning Annapurna massif.

What is the maximum altitude during the ABC Short Trek?

∨
The highest point is Annapurna Base Camp at 4,130 meters (13,550 feet). While not extreme, trekkers should be aware of basic altitude precautions.

What permits do I need for the ABC Short Trek?

∨
ACAP (Annapurna Conservation Area Permit) TIMS (Trekkers’ Information Management System) card Nepal Unique Treks takes care of all permit arrangements for you.

What is the best time for the ABC Short Trek?

∨
The best seasons are: Spring (March to May) – Great weather and blooming rhododendrons Autumn (September to November) – Clear skies and excellent mountain views Trekking is possible year-round except during the monsoon (June–August) when trails are slippery and leech-prone.

What kind of accommodation is available on the trek?

∨
You’ll stay in mountain teahouses, which offer basic but clean twin-sharing rooms, blankets, and shared toilets. Most villages along the route have well-maintained lodges with warm dining halls.

What kind of food is served during the trek?

∨
Teahouses offer a variety of freshly prepared meals such as Dal Bhat (rice, lentils, vegetables), noodles, soups, fried rice, pasta, pancakes, and omelets. Vegetarian options are widely available. Meals are carb-heavy to fuel trekking at high altitude.

Do I need a guide and porter?

∨
Yes, Nepal Unique Treks highly recommends trekking with a licensed guide for safety and cultural insight. A porter can carry your heavy backpack (up to 12 kg), allowing you to trek with just a light daypack.

Is altitude sickness a concern on this trek?

∨
While the risk is lower than on higher Himalayan treks, some trekkers may experience mild symptoms such as headaches or shortness of breath. Staying hydrated, ascending gradually, and listening to your guide help minimize risks.

What should I pack for the ABC Short Trek?

∨
Important items include: Good quality trekking boots Layered clothing (thermal base, fleece, down jacket) Rain gear Hat, gloves, and sunglasses Trekking poles (optional but helpful) Personal first aid Sleeping bag rated to -10°C (can be rented) A detailed checklist will be provided by Nepal Unique Treks upon booking.

What is included in the ABC short trek package?

∨
Our standard package includes: Trekking permits (ACAP & TIMS) Licensed English-speaking guide and porter All meals (breakfast, lunch, dinner) and accommodation during the trek Ground transportation (Pokhara–trek start point–return) First aid kit Government taxes and service charges Additional services like airport transfers and hotel stays in Pokhara can be arranged upon request.

How do I book the trek?

∨
You can book via our official website, email, or WhatsApp. A deposit secures your spot, and the remaining balance can be paid upon arrival. We accept various payment options, including cash, bank transfers, and credit cards.

Is there Wi-Fi on the Annapurna Base Camp trail?

∨
Yes, Wi-Fi is available in most villages along the trail for a small fee, especially in popular stops like Chhomrong, Himalaya, and ABC itself. Connection quality varies and is often slower at higher altitudes. Most lodges charge around NPR 300–500 for limited access.

Can I use a mobile network during the trek?

∨
Mobile coverage is fairly good in lower areas. NTC and Ncell networks work in many villages along the route. However, reception may be weak or unavailable at higher elevations like Annapurna Base Camp. Consider buying a local SIM card in Pokhara before starting the trek.

Should I tip my guide and porter?

∨
Tipping is not mandatory but is greatly appreciated in Nepal’s trekking culture. A general guideline is USD 10-20 per day for the guide, USD 10 per day for the porter The total amount can be adjusted based on your satisfaction and group size.

Can I extend the trek or combine it with other activities?

∨
Yes! Nepal Unique Treks can help you extend your trip. Popular add-ons include: Poon Hill Sunrise Trek Hot springs at Jhinu Danda Sightseeing in Pokhara or Kathmandu Adventure activities like paragliding, rafting, or jungle safari in Chitwan We can fully customize your itinerary based on your time and interests.
