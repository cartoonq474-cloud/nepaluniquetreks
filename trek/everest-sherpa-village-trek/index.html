Everest Sherpa Village Trek is a popular trekking route in the Khumbu region. people called Everest View Trek, Everest Mini Trek, and the Everest Panorama Trek. Here we break down the Cost & Itinerary. But we are introducing our clients as an Everest Sherpa Village Trek, because there are many Sherpas who were born who received awards like World Record Holders.

When Sir Edmund Hillary climbed in 1953, many local Sherpa were involved as supporters. and Late Tenzing Norgay Sherpa was the main guide who led expeditions. And still, Sherpa culture, traditions, and Buddhist religion are preserved by the community and the Nepalese government.

Everest Sherpa Village Trek starts from Lukla Airport to the Khumjung Village via Phakding, Manjo, and Namche Bazaar. This village is the beginning of the trek to the Everest Base Camp Trek. Through Namche Bazaar, we can go to Renjola Pass via Thame Village, and there are many alternative trekking routes to go to Everest Base Camp Chola Pass Gokyo Trek. Which way to do trekking and how long you want to do trekking will depend on your Budget and vacation. But most of the visitors choose the Everest Base Camp regular trek and the Gokyo Chola Pass Everest Base Camp Trek.

During the Everest Sherpa Village trek, explore the local village and stay in a Normal Tea house, which helps the Sherpa community indirectly. Also, if you need Luxury accommodation or Normal type of Guesthouses, there are options, depending on your budget, you may choose your Trekking Package.

How difficult is the Everest Sherpa Village Trek?

Everest Sherpa Village Trek is a moderate trekking destination in the Everest region, but it is an easier trek to compare Everest Base Camp, Gokyo Valley Trek, or High Pass Trek with Everest Base Camp. Treks start from 2850m above sea level and go up to 3900m. The 1st-day trek from Lukla to Phakding is easy, but the second day is quite hard and steep up from Phakding to Namche. After Namche, you will explore villages, which is easier than the Phakding to Namche Trek.

Namche Bazaar is located at 3440m, where every trekker stays 2 nights for acclimatization and goes higher to continue to Everest Base Camp. Also, Namche Bazaar is a type of modern city where many facilities for trekkers, like a luxury Hotel, Shopping, Night Music, Massage, coffee shop, and many foreign restaurants with the Everest Documentary Show.

Everest Sherpa Village Trek Itinerary: Day by Day

8 Days program for Everest Sherpa Village Trek. Daily timings and sequences remain flexible according to weather, flight operations, and group pace.

Day 1: Drive from Kathmandu to Ramechhap 5/6 hrs, Flight to Lukla (2,850m) and Trek to Phakding (2,650m

Approx. 2,650 m / 8,694 ft

Duration: 5/6 hrs
Accommodation: Guesthouse
Meals: Breakfast, Lunch and Dinner

Your Everest Sherpa Village Trek begins with 5/6 hrs drive trough himalayan local village to Ramechhap and from there an exciting mountain flight to Lukla, known as the gateway to the Everest region. During the scenic Himalayan flight, enjoy breathtaking aerial views of snow-capped peaks, deep valleys, and traditional mountain villages. After landing at Lukla Airport, you will start trekking through beautiful pine forests, suspension bridges, and Sherpa settlements along the Dudh Koshi River. Passing small villages like Chheplung and Ghat, you will arrive at the peaceful village of Phakding. Phakding is a charming riverside village surrounded by Himalayan scenery and traditional Sherpa culture, making it an ideal place for your first overnight stay in the Everest region. Highlights Scenic Kathmandu to Lukla mountain flight First views of the Everest Himalayan range Traditional Sherpa villages and suspension bridges Comfortable guest house accommodation

Day 2: Trek from Phakding to Namche Bazaar (3,440m) Trek Distance 12 km, Walking Duration 6 hours

Approx. 3,440 m / 11,286 ft

Duration: 6 hours
Distance: 12 km
Accommodation: Guesthouse
Meals: Breakfast, Lunch and Dinner

After breakfast, continue trekking alongside the Dudh Koshi River through beautiful rhododendron and pine forests. Cross several famous suspension bridges, including the iconic Hillary Suspension Bridge. The trail enters Sagarmatha National Park, a UNESCO World Heritage Site known for rare wildlife, dramatic landscapes, and stunning mountain scenery. After a gradual ascent, you will climb steeply toward Namche Bazaar, the main trading hub of the Everest region. Namche Bazaar is a vibrant Sherpa town filled with bakeries, cafes, trekking shops, and panoramic viewpoints. If the weather is clear, you may enjoy your first distant view of Mount Everest along with Lhotse and Ama Dablam. Highlights Entrance to Sagarmatha National Park Hillary Suspension Bridge Spectacular Himalayan mountain views Explore Namche Bazaar, the Sherpa town

Day 3: rek from Namche Bazaar to Thame Village (3,800m) Trek Distance 8 km, Walking Duration 3 to 4 hours

Approx. 3,800 m / 12,467 ft

Duration: 4 hours
Distance: 8 km
Accommodation: Guesthouse
Meals: Breakfast, Lunch and Dinner

Today’s trail follows an ancient Tibetan trade route toward the traditional Sherpa village of Thame. The path offers peaceful trekking away from the busier Everest Base Camp trail. You will pass through scenic landscapes, Buddhist prayer walls, chortens, and beautiful mountain valleys surrounded by towering Himalayan peaks. Thame Village is famous for its rich Sherpa heritage and ancient monastery. This hidden Himalayan village provides a unique cultural experience and incredible mountain scenery. Highlights Traditional Sherpa mountain village Ancient Buddhist culture and monasteries Quiet and scenic Everest trekking route Beautiful Himalayan landscapes

Day 4: Trek from Thame to Khumjung Village (3,790m) Trek Distance 9 km, Walking Duration 4 hours

Approx. 3,790 m / 12,434 ft

Duration: 4 hours
Distance: 9 km
Accommodation: Guesthouse
Meals: Breakfast, Lunch and Dinner

After breakfast, trek through beautiful Himalayan terrain toward Khumjung, one of the largest Sherpa settlements in the Everest region. Khumjung is famous for its stone-built houses, potato farms, Buddhist monastery, and the historic Hillary School established by Sir Edmund Hillary. The village offers incredible views of Ama Dablam, Thamserku, and Khumbila Peak. You may also visit the famous Khumjung Monastery, which is believed to preserve a legendary Yeti scalp. Highlights Authentic Sherpa lifestyle Khumjung Monastery and Hillary School Panoramic Himalayan mountain views Peaceful Everest trekking experience

Day 5: Trek from Khumjung to Tengboche Monastery (3,860m) Trek Distance 10 km, Walking Duration 5 hours

Approx. 3,860 m / 12,664 ft

Duration: 5 hours
Distance: 10 km
Accommodation: Guesthouse
Meals: Breakfast, Lunch and Dinner

Today you will trek through rhododendron forests and scenic mountain trails toward the famous Tengboche Monastery, the largest Buddhist monastery in the Everest region. The trail provides magnificent views of Everest, Ama Dablam, Nuptse, and Lhotse. Tengboche is one of the most spiritual places in the Khumbu region and is surrounded by spectacular Himalayan scenery. In the evening, you may attend Buddhist prayer ceremonies and enjoy peaceful mountain surroundings. Highlights Tengboche Monastery visit Panoramic Everest Himalayan views Buddhist spiritual experience Rhododendron forest trekking trail

Day 6: Trek from Tengboche to Phakding (2,650m) Trek Distance 14 km, Walking Duration 6 hours

Approx. 2,650 m / 8,694 ft

Duration: 6 hours
Distance: 14 km
Accommodation: Guesthouse
Meals: Breakfast, Lunch and Dinner

After enjoying sunrise mountain views, descend through forests, suspension bridges, and Sherpa villages toward Phakding. The downhill trek offers another opportunity to experience local culture, beautiful riverside scenery, and Himalayan landscapes. Highlights Scenic descent through the Everest region Traditional Sherpa villages Beautiful forest and river trails Comfortable overnight stay in Phakding

Day 7: Trek from Phakding to Lukla (2,850m) Trek Distance 8 km, Walking Duration 4 hours

Approx. 2,850 m / 9,350 ft

Duration: 4 hours
Distance: 8 km
Accommodation: Guesthouse
Meals: Breakfast, Lunch and Dinner

Your final trekking day follows the Dudh Koshi River back to Lukla. Enjoy your last moments in the Everest region while celebrating the successful completion of your Himalayan adventure. Spend the evening relaxing and exploring Lukla town. Highlights Final day of the Everest trekking Scenic Himalayan landscapes Celebration dinner in Lukla Traditional mountain hospitality

Day 8: Flight from Lukla to Ramechhap and drive to Kathmandu 132 km, 5/6 hrs

Approx. 1,400 m / 4,593 ft

Duration: 5/6 hrs
Distance: 132 km
Accommodation: Standard Guesthouse
Meals: Breakfast

Early in the morning, you will take a scenic mountain flight from Lukla to Ramechhap Airport. During peak trekking seasons in Nepal, Lukla flights are often operated through Ramechhap due to air traffic management in Kathmandu. The short but spectacular flight offers breathtaking views of the Himalayan mountain range, deep valleys, rivers, and traditional mountain villages. Upon arrival at Ramechhap, you will begin a comfortable drive back to Kathmandu through beautiful countryside landscapes, terraced farms, rivers, and local villages. After reaching Kathmandu, you can relax at your hotel or spend your free time exploring the vibrant streets, local markets, UNESCO World Heritage Sites, cafes, and cultural attractions of Nepal’s capital city. Highlights Scenic Lukla to Ramechhap mountain flight Beautiful countryside drive to Kathmandu Himalayan landscapes and river valleys Comfortable transfer after the Everest trekking adventure
