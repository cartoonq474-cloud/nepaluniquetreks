Overview of Ghorepani Poon Hill Trek

The Ghorepani Poon Hill Trek is one of the most popular and rewarding short treks in Nepal, famous for its breathtaking Himalayan sunrise views, traditional Gurung and Magar villages, and scenic trails through rhododendron forests. Organized by Nepal Unique Treks, a trusted local trekking agency based in Kathmandu, the Poon Hill Trek is perfect for beginners, families, and anyone looking for an easy yet scenic trek in Nepal. The trek takes you to Poon Hill (3,210 m), one of the best viewpoints in the country, where you can witness a spectacular sunrise over Annapurna I, Dhaulagiri, Machhapuchhre (Fishtail), Annapurna South, and many other snow-capped peaks.

Starting from Pokhara, the Ghorepani Poon Hill Trek combines comfortable walking days, well-maintained trails, cozy teahouses, and rich local culture. Why the Ghorepani Poon Hill Trek Is One of the Best Short Treks in Nepal

The Ghorepani Poon Hill Trek is one of the most scenic and accessible treks in the Annapurna region of Nepal, making it a top choice for travelers looking for a short yet rewarding Himalayan adventure. Known for its iconic sunrise viewpoint, charming mountain villages, and forested trails, this trek offers a perfect introduction to trekking in Nepal without extreme altitude or technical challenges.

Highlights of the Ghorepani Poon Hill Trek

Poon Hill Sunrise View (3,210 m)

The sunrise view from Poon Hill is one of the most photographed mountain scenes in Nepal. Early in the morning, golden light slowly illuminates Annapurna I, Dhaulagiri, Machhapuchhre (Fishtail), Annapurna South, and Nilgiri, creating a breathtaking Himalayan panorama.

Himalayan Views of Annapurna & Dhaulagiri Range

Despite being a short trek, the Ghorepani Poon Hill route offers uninterrupted views of some of Nepal’s highest peaks, making it one of the best viewpoint treks in Nepal.

Traditional Gurung and Magar Villages

The trek passes through authentic Gurung and Magar villages such as Ulleri, Ghorepani, and Ghandruk, allowing trekkers to experience local culture, traditions, and warm Himalayan hospitality.

Rhododendron Forests and Stone Stair Trails

In spring, the trail is filled with blooming rhododendron forests, while stone staircases and terraced farmlands add to the trek’s scenic charm.

Ghorepani Poon Hill Trek Itinerary

Typical 4–5 Days Ghorepani Poon Hill Trek Itinerary

Most trekkers choose a 4 or 5-day Poon Hill Trek itinerary from Pokhara to Pokhara, making it perfect for those with limited holidays. If we count from Arrivel to till departure, it will be 8 Days and 9 nights.

Typical Route:

Kathmandu – Pokhara – Nayapul – Tikhedhunga/Ulleri – Ghorepani – Poon Hill – Tadapani/Ghandruk – Pokhara – Kathmandu

Extended Ghorepani Poon Hill Trek Options

For those wanting more walking days, the trek can be extended to include Tadapani, Ghandruk, or combined with the Annapurna Base Camp Trek.

Ghorepani Poon Hill Trek Itinerary: Day by Day

9 Days program for Ghorepani Poon Hill Trek. Daily timings and sequences remain flexible according to weather, flight operations, and group pace.

Day 1: Arrive at Kathmandu (1345m): transfer to hotel and overnight at hotel

Approx. 1,345 m / 4,413 ft

Accommodation: 3-star Hotel

Nepal Unique Treks staff will receive you at the Tribhuwan International Airport in Kathmandu and bring you to the hotel. If you arrive earlier time on the day we have time to discuss about our trip.

Day 2: Drive from Kathmandu to Pokhara 210 km, 810 M

Approx. 810 m / 2,657 ft

Distance: 210 km
Accommodation: 3-star Hotel
Meals: Breakfast

Early in the morning, your Trek guide will come to your Hotel. After having breakfast, a few minutes' hikes to the tourist bus station. 7/8 hrs drive to arrive in Pokhara. It will depend on highway traffic conditions. Tourist buses will drive through beautiful highways, white water rafting rivers, and local villages, The driver will stop for breakfast, tea, coffee, and lunch at noon on the way. You will enjoy the day with beautiful scenery. Early afternoon, you will be in Pokhara. Check in the Hotel. Take a rest and be fresh. In the evening, you are free to explore the lakeside of Pokhara and enjoy dinner.

Day 3: Drive from Pokhara to Nayapul 1050 M, Trek to Tikhedhunga 1525 M

Approx. 1,525 m / 5,003 ft

Accommodation: Guesthouse
Meals: Breakfast, Lunch and Dinner

After breakfast, drive from Pokhara to Nayapul, where you will be introduced to our local porter and change your trekking gear. Time to buy drinking water, put on sunscreen, take Toilet breaks, and manage your day bag. Our trek starts from Nayapul, heading towards Tikhedhunga at 1525m. You will short walk along the Modi river, there is a small, beautiful village called Birethanti with many shops and trekking guesthouses. Then the path starts slightly climbing up the valley to Hile Village, and then Tikhedhunga. Overnight stay at the guesthouse.

Day 4: Trek from Tikhedhunga to Ghorepani 2875 M, 6/7 hrs trek.

Approx. 2,875 m / 9,432 ft

Duration: 6/7 hrs
Accommodation: Guesthouse
Meals: Breakfast, Lunch and Dinner

Early morning, your guide will arrange a hot breakfast. Then start the trek with a steep ascent to Ulleri village, 1960m Ulleri village has a large ethnic group of people, Magar. They have interesting cultures and speak the Magar language. And the trek continues through forests of oak and rhododendron until Banthanti village at 2250m. Your guide will stop there to have lunch. After that, head toward Nangethanti 2460m, and then Ghorepani 2875m). From there, you will be surprised Annapurna sunset view. Overnight stay in the Guesthouse.

Day 5: Trek from Ghorepani to Tadapani 2610 M, 6 hrs trek. Visit Poon Hill Viewpoint 3210.

Approx. 2,610 m / 8,563 ft

Duration: 6 hrs
Accommodation: Guesthouse
Meals: Breakfast, Lunch and Dinner

Early morning, around 4 am, your guide will take you to the Poonhill viewpoint, from there you will discover the panoramic Mt Annapurna view with a magnificent sunrise view. Spend time with warm coffee and Hotchoklet. In the spring session, you will see many types of Rhododendron Nepali National flowers. if you are earlier winter you will see the different beauty of the Himalayas. Return to the Ghorepani for breakfast before trekking to Tadapani (2610m).

Day 6: Trek from Tadapani to Ghandruk Village. 1940 M, 4 hrs

Approx. 1,940 m / 6,365 ft

Duration: 4 hrs
Accommodation: Guesthouse
Meals: Breakfast, Lunch and Dinner

Today is the last day trek from where you can see Mt fishtail and Mt Annapurna South. Before breakfast, you will enjoy with dramatic sunrise view. After breakfast, trek into the rainforest through Bhaisekharka. Descend to the river within a few hrs, you will be rich in Ghandruk Village. Today shorter trek, giving you plenty of time to explore Ghandruk village, one of the largest Gurung villages. where you can introduce yourself to the large community of the Gorkha Army. There you will see highlights of the Gurung Culture, religion, and lifestyle of an earlier generation. The museum gives an insight into the lives of the people. With the backdrop of snowy peaks, rest are welcome breaks for today.

Day 7: Drive from Ghandruk to Pokhara 810. 3 hrs drive.

Approx. 810 m / 2,657 ft

Duration: 3 hrs
Accommodation: 3-star Hotel
Meals: Breakfast, Lunch

Today you will enjoy the final breakfast of the trek before trekking through villages to reach Birethanti, your guide will enter trekking permits to exit Annapurna Nationalpark. Trek to Nayapul from where you can take a bus or jeep back to Pokhara. Last Night in Pokhara enjoy the bright lights of the bars and restaurants in Pokhara and gaze once again at the lake and up at Fishtail Mountain.

Day 8: Drive from Pokhara to Kathmandu 210 km, 7/8 hrs

Approx. 1,345 m / 4,413 ft

Duration: 7/8 hrs
Distance: 210 km
Accommodation: 3-star Hotel
Meals: Breakfast, Dinner

At 7 am in the morning, your guide will take you to the tourist bus station. maximum 8 hrs drive to rich Kathmandu, but sometimes you can rich in a few hrs early or a few hrs late of highway traffic conditions. Nepal Unique Treks will invite you to join us for a farewell dinner with a Nepali cultural show, and a test of Nepali real food.

Day 9: Departure to International Airport.

Approx. 1,345 m / 4,413 ft

Accommodation: Hotel, Lodge & Tea House
Meals: Breakfast

Our trek finishes today. Our air staff will drop you at the universal air terminal in Kathmandu for your flight take-off from Nepal.

Frequently Asked Questions for Ghorepani Poon Hill Trek

How difficult is the Ghorepani Poon Hill Trek?

∨
The Ghorepani Poon Hill Trek difficulty is rated easy to moderate, making it one of the best treks in Nepal for beginners. The trail includes stone staircases and gradual uphill walks, but no technical climbing is required. With a normal fitness level and proper pacing, most trekkers can comfortably complete the trek.

What is the maximum altitude of the Poon Hill Trek?

∨
The highest point of the trek is Poon Hill at 3,210 meters (10,531 feet). Because the altitude is relatively low compared to other Himalayan treks, the risk of altitude sickness is minimal. Nepal Unique Treks plans the itinerary with gradual ascents to ensure comfort and safety.

How many days are needed for the Ghorepani Poon Hill Trek?

∨
The trek usually takes 4 to 7 days, depending on the route and walking pace. The most popular option is a 4 or 5-day Poon Hill Trek from Pokhara, while longer itineraries include Ghandruk or Tadapani for a deeper cultural experience.

What is the best time to do the Ghorepani Poon Hill Trek?

∨
The best time for the Ghorepani Poon Hill Trek is during spring (March to May) and autumn (September to November). Spring offers blooming rhododendron forests, while autumn provides clear skies and stable weather. The trek is also possible in winter and monsoon with proper planning.

Is the Poon Hill Trek suitable for beginners and families?

∨
Yes, the Poon Hill Trek is ideal for beginners, families, and senior travelers. The moderate altitude, short walking days, and comfortable teahouse accommodation make it a perfect family-friendly trek in Nepal when guided by experienced professionals like Nepal Unique Treks.

How far is the Ghorepani Poon Hill Trek?

∨
The total Ghorepani Poon Hill Trek distance is approximately 40–50 kilometers, depending on the itinerary. Daily walking hours range from 4 to 6 hours, allowing enough time for rest, photography, and cultural interaction.

Do I need a guide for the Ghorepani Poon Hill Trek?

∨
While it is possible to trek independently, hiring a licensed local guide is highly recommended. Trekking with Nepal Unique Treks ensures safety, smooth logistics, cultural insights, and support in case of weather changes or emergencies.

What permits are required for the Ghorepani Poon Hill Trek?

∨
Trekkers need two permits: Annapurna Conservation Area Permit (ACAP) TIMS Card (Trekkers’ Information Management System) Nepal Unique Treks arranges all required permits before the trek begins.

What kind of accommodation is available on the Poon Hill Trek?

∨
Accommodation is provided in local teahouses, offering clean rooms, warm blankets, and shared or private bathrooms in some locations. Staying in teahouses also supports local communities along the Ghorepani trekking route.

What food is available during the Ghorepani Poon Hill Trek?

∨
Teahouses serve a variety of meals, including Dal Bhat, noodles, pasta, soups, momos, rice dishes, pancakes, and breakfast items. Meals are freshly prepared and provide enough energy for daily trekking.

Is altitude sickness a concern on the Poon Hill Trek?

∨
Altitude sickness is rare on the Poon Hill Trek due to the moderate elevation. However, proper hydration, gradual walking, and listening to your guide’s advice help ensure a safe trekking experience.

Can the Ghorepani Poon Hill Trek be done in winter?

∨
Yes, the trek can be done in winter (December to February). Winter offers clear mountain views and fewer crowds, though mornings can be cold. Nepal Unique Treks provides proper guidance and seasonal itinerary adjustments for winter trekking.
