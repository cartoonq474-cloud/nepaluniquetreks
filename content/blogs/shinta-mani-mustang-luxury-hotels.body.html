Shinta Mani Mustang has been ranked the world's No. 1 hotel in Robb Report's 50 Greatest Luxury Hotels on Earth 2026,

Nepal has recived another remarkable milestone in the global tourism industry as Shinta Mani Mustang has been recognized as the World's No. 1 Luxury Hotel in Robb Report's "50 Greatest Luxury Hotels on Earth 2026." The prestigious recognition highlights Nepal's growing reputation as a destination that offers not only world-class trekking adventures but also exceptional luxury travel experiences.

Located in the spectacular landscapes of Lower Mustang, Shinta Mani Mustang has earned international praise for its outstanding hospitality, sustainable luxury, breathtaking Himalayan scenery, and authentic cultural experiences. The ranking was selected by Robb Report's Travel Masters, a panel of leading luxury travel experts from around the world who evaluate the finest hotels based on service, design, guest experience, and destination appeal.

A Proud Moment for Nepal Tourism

For decades, Nepal has been internationally recognized for iconic trekking destinations such as Everest Base Camp Trek, the Annapurna Circuit Trek, Upper Mustang Trek, and the Manaslu Circuit Trek. This latest achievement demonstrates that Nepal is also emerging as a premier destination for luxury travelers seeking exclusive Himalayan experiences.

The international recognition is expected to attract more high-end travelers who wish to combine luxury accommodation with authentic Himalayan culture, ancient monasteries, wellness experiences, and breathtaking mountain landscapes.

Why Mustang Continues to Captivate Travelers

Mustang remains one of Nepal's most extraordinary destinations because of its unique combination of natural beauty, Tibetan Buddhist heritage, and remote Himalayan lifestyle. Visitors can experience:

  • Ancient monasteries and centuries-old Buddhist traditions
  • The historic walled city of Lo Manthang
  • Dramatic desert landscapes beneath the Himalayas
  • Colorful Tibetan culture and festivals
  • Scenic jeep tours and trekking adventures
  • Authentic local villages and traditional hospitality

Whether travelers choose a luxury lodge or a classic trekking adventure, Mustang offers unforgettable experiences unlike anywhere else in Nepal.

What This Means for Nepal's Tourism Industry

International awards like this strengthen Nepal's image as a world-class travel destination. They also help promote sustainable tourism by encouraging visitors to explore lesser-known regions while supporting local communities, businesses, and conservation efforts.

The recognition of a luxury property in Mustang also creates new opportunities for tourism entrepreneurs, trekking agencies, local guides, and hospitality providers throughout the region.
