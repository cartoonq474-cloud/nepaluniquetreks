Nepali cuisine is diverse and reflects the country's geography and cultural diversity. Here are some traditional Nepali dishes:

  1. Dal Bhat: This is the staple food of Nepal, consisting of steamed rice and lentil soup (dal). It is usually served with various side dishes such as vegetables, pickles, and curries. Also, local people known as Thakali Thali set for Lunch and Dinner. 

  2. Momos: These are Nepali dumplings made with flour dough and filled with minced meat or vegetables. They are usually served with a spicy dipping sauce.

  3. Aloo Tama: This is a traditional Nepali curry made with bamboo shoots (tama) and potatoes (aloo) in a tomato-based sauce.

  4. Sel Roti: It is a sweet, deep-fried rice flour doughnut-like bread that is popular during festivals in Nepal.

  5. Gundruk: It is a fermented leafy vegetable, usually made from mustard greens, that is used in various Nepali dishes.

  6. Kwati: This is a soup made with a mixture of nine different types of sprouted beans and is traditionally eaten during the festival of Janai Purnima.

  7. Chatamari: It is a thin crepe-like dish made with rice flour, topped with minced meat, vegetables, and eggs.

  8. Thukpa: It is a hearty noodle soup made with vegetables, meat, and noodles that is popular in the mountainous regions of Nepal.

These are just a few examples of traditional Nepali dishes. Nepal's cuisine has a lot to offer, and there are many more delicious dishes to discover.
