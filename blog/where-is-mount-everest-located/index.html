Where Is Mount Everest Located?

By Dil Gurung • Founder & Managing Director • 📅 September 22, 2026 • ⏱️ 12 MIN READ
Table of Contents (7) ▾
  • Is Mount Everest in Nepal or Tibet?
  • How High Is Mount Everest?
  • Where Is Everest Base Camp?
  • How Do You Get to Mount Everest From Nepal?
  • Can You Visit Mount Everest Without Climbing It?
  • How Can You Climb Mount Everest?
  • Best Time to Visit Mount Everest

Mt. Everest is in the Himalayas Region, on the border between Nepal and Tibet. It is in Mahalangur Himalayan Range, where big peaks, deep valleys, and high altitude mountain passes enhances the landscape. Its southern side faces Nepal which is also known as main face, while the northern side faces Tibet.

For people asking Where Is Mount Everest, the direct answer is Nepal as the main face is in Nepal. But we can say that summit sits on international border of Nepal and Tibet. Its’ falls within Solukhumbu district in Nepal eastern part. This part is famous for Sherpa communities and Khumbu Valley. 

In Nepal side, its more connected with trekking. Most travel start from Kathmandu to the Khumbu Region and the long walk starts towards mountain. They way passes through small villages, amazing suspension bridges, green forests and wide open valleys. The first major stop Namche bazaar is one of the best stop to explore and start the trekking.

As a trekker proceed higher altitude, the scenes changes rapidly. We can see fewer trees, the air in that altitude becomes thinner, and the big mountain feel much closer. We will be witnessing the Ama Dalam, Lhotse, Nuptse, and Pumori are visible along with the trial. For almost every people, this all views are super special part of the Everest Base Camp Trekking Experience.

The other side, also known as Tibetan side, gives trekkers another side view of the mountain. The northern side can be reached via Tibet, which is covered by road by most place, which requires separate permits and arrangements because Tibet have its own rules and regulations for entry of visitors.

Also, Mt. Everest is part of greater Himalayan range, which covers many countries in Asia. And, Himalayas are home to many popular and amazing highest mountains which are shaping the culture and lives of communities living in these uphill valleys for the generation to generation.

The main location of Everest is very important for all trekkers and climbers. Visitors from around the world can choose a very nice trekking journey to experience the big mountain from very close range. Also, mountaineers preparing for the summit attempt need to follow a very demanding route, with more technical climbing, acclimatization, and very careful planning becomes the part of daily life.

Is Mount Everest in Nepal or Tibet?

We can say that Mount Everest is on the border between Nepal and Tibet. So, people will find it hard to give Everest just to Nepal or to Tibet. The main summit of the Everest lies on the Nepal-Tibet border, with mountain extending on the both sides.

When the query rise, where is Mount Everest, the main focus depends on which side they are asking about, if they are talking about southern side its, Nepal and it they are talking about northern side its Tibet. Moreover, Nepal side is located in solukhumbu district which is the part of Khumbu region.

Also, Nepal side is more famous for trekking experiences. Many travellers start with Kathmandu to Everest Region and go through the trial of namche bazaar, tengboche, dingboche and lobuche. They routes takes them with the journey of passes through valleys, green and dense forest, big and standing amazing mountains landscapes, and ancient Sherpa settlements. And importantly, Everest base camp on this side is one of the best and popular trekking destinations of the Nepal.

The other side known as Tibetan side offers completely different experience. Travellers around the world can also approach Everest from North side and visit the Everest north base camp. There is much closer road access to the main base camp than a usual trekking route from southern side. Also, travelling from Tibetan side need much more documentation and permits and organized arrangements.

Also, there is vast difference between the feelings of both approaches. Normally Southern side also known as Nepal route is strongly connected with long walking hours, monasteries, Sherpa settlements, and rich culture and trekking. Other route has a more open landscape and road access to people who travels by that side.

We can agree that both side views are impressive, but they both are not the same experiences. The major difference is whether traveller wants the long trekking route through high Himalayan range or different journey toward the Everest via Tibet route.

But to confirm, for all trekkers, Nepal side has a special meaning. The route doesn’t simply lead to a famous Everest mountain. It takes visitors through the local communities where generations of Sherpa people have lived , worked  , and built their lives among the highest mountain that exist in the Earth.

How High Is Mount Everest?

Mount Everest is 8848.86 meters high above sea level or we can say it is 29031.69 feet high above sea level. The provided height is official height of the mountain, jointly declared by Nepal and China in 2020. The measurement includes the snow and the ice covering summit, which may change slightly within the time because of the different natural conditions.

Everest is the highest and famous mountain in the world above the sea level. Also, it attracts the more trekkers, climbers, researchers, adventurers, and wonderers throughout the world. And, when people say where is mount Everest, the height is also the one of the other factor that people want to know.

As we know measuring the mountain is not the easy task. Survey team with hi tech equipment, GPS technology, satellite data and other different precise methods to calculate the elevation of the Everest. Before the measurement present slightly different data, but the enhanced technology has allowed scientists to make more precise calculation.

The actual summit is located on the Nepal-Tibet border in the himalayan region. At such harsh and extreme condition and altitude, the environment becomes so unpredictable. The temperature can drop far below the freezing point, air are thin and oxygen levels are much lower than at sea level, also the strong winds can make the climbing condition more dangerous.

For the trekkers, reaching Everest base camp doesn’t mean reaching to the summit. Everest base camp is the much lower area than the top of the Everest. It can be reached without any technical expertise or climbing experience. A summit expedition is completely different challenge and that requires lots of efforts and technical skill of climbing. Also it needs much more skills, acclimatization, special equipment and intense preparation.

The major difference in altitude can be felt during the journey. As trekkers move the higher altitude in the Khumbu region, the air become thin and walking becomes slower and tougher. Every uphill section requires the extensive effort.

Where Is Everest Base Camp?

As we have already discussed earlier, the main two Everest base camp lies in nepal and Tibet, southern side in nepal and northern side in Tibet side. And both serve as an important starting point for the climbers for the attempt to Everest summit.

And, for the travellers asking where is mount Everest, knowing the location of the base camp also matters, many people are looking for the information to understand the Everest base camp and Everest mountain. The southern base camp is in Khumbu region, Solukhumbu District , Nepal and northern base camp is in Tibet.

Most of the people start their journey to base camp from Kathmandu, as Kathmandu is the capital city and most accessible city of Nepal with the oldest international airport of nepal, known as Tribhuwan International Airport. Then the journey start to Lukla basically from Kathmandu or manthali via domestic flight.

Normally the walk or trek is not only about reaching the final destination. Along the trekking way, the visitors cross the suspension bridges, walking around the rivers, pass the Buddhist monasteries with Buddhist culture, and meet local communities that serve the place and make it nicer to live. The landscapes keep on changing as trail goes higher. Slowly the tree line disappears and visitors start seeking clearly more rocky valleys and snow covered peaks.

Everest Base Camp itself lies at 5364 meters above the sea level. From the base camp, trekkers can see the surrounding others Himalayan landscape, although the summit is not fully visible from the camp. To enhance the experience the trekkers continue to Kalapathhar for wider view of the Everest and surroundings peaks.

Now, the other sides give the totally different experience. Travellers can reach much closer of northern side by a road access , but special permit and travel arrangements are required. The nepal route is associated with classic Everest base camp trekking experience and the long and adventurous walk through the Khumbu Valley.

How Do You Get to Mount Everest From Nepal?

Getting to Mount Everest from Nepal side is an adventure journey that takes time, hard walking, and some careful planning. Travellers cannot simply drive or take a flight directly to the mountain. Most of the visitors first arrive to Kathmandu and then travel towards the Everest region located at eastern Nepal.

The classic route starts with a flight to Lukla, a small but great town that serve as a great connecting point to the Khumbu region. From Lukla, trekkers start their walking journey passing through the villages, suspension bridges, forests, and narrow and steep paths.

The other classic town is namche bazaar , the most important stop, where people spend one extra night to acclimatize which helps them to adjust with the increasing altitude. From the Namche, the routes leads to Tengboche, Dingboche, Lobuche and eventually to the Everest Base Camp.

The journey to Everest base camp is not only about the covering distance. As a visitor climbs higher, the views and landscapes changes. Green hills suddenly changes to rocky valleys, while larger himalayan comes close to the view. Local culture will welcome you and monastaries will add more real value to your journey.

For people looking information on where is mount Everest, it is useful to know about the mountain itself is very far from road network. Reaching its base from nepal side requires to trek through the khumbu valley. This is one reason that Everest journey is special and unique from other mountain trip.

The precise travel arrangements can vary depending on the itinerary selected, season, weather, flight operation and the other natural factors that we cannot control. Some routes also use road travel towards Everest region before starting the actual treks. Regardless the way selected , reaching the Everest from Nepal means entering a high mountain environment where the travel journey becomes the part for the beautiful experience.

Can You Visit Mount Everest Without Climbing It?

Obviously yes, you can get the experience of visiting the Mount Everest without climbing it or without its summit. In general, most of the travellers who visit Everest region are trekkers as climbing needs a lot of technical expertise and discipline. You don’t need to be an experienced mountaineer to walk in Khumbu region.  You can enjoy the view of highest mountains on the earth by just walking around it for several days with great views.

The Everest base camp trekking is one of the most popular and good way to experience the mountain from Nepal. The trail passes through different experiences like Sherpa village, Buddhist culture, monasteries, suspension bridges, and high mountain valleys. Each day will bring you the different excited views.

If you are also wondering that where is mount Everest, the Nepal side has lot more to offer in Everest section.  The journey take you side view of Nuptse, Pumori, and other surrounding peaks. Also the other main view point is Kala Patthar, where you will get more clear views of different peaks.

Travelling to Everest without climbing gives you the time to explore more local culture , understand the Sherpa civilization and their way of living in mountains give you more stories to listen and understand. You will understand more about traditional Sherpa home, traditional prayer wheels, mani walls and monasteries.

For many visitors, the ultimate goal is not to sand on the summit. Walking beneath the highest mountain of the world, reaching Everest base camp, and watching the first ray of the light touching the surrounding peaks can be more powerful memory without the risk and demand of full Everest expedition.

How Can You Climb Mount Everest?

Climbing the world highest mountain is very difficult and different from visiting Everest Base Camp. Reaching the base camp involves high altitude trekking and understanding the trekking route while reaching the summit require extensive mountaineering skills, strong preparation, and more experience in the more extreme condition. It is a long journey and practice that may take several weeks to months.

Before starting the actual climb, the climbers need to spend the months preparing their body and mind for the demand of higher altitude. They also need the experience with the use of crampons, ropes , rope knots, ice axes, and other highly skill full climbing equipment’s. Also, previous experience on high mountains plays important role.

They also need to have the climbing permits and must be arranged a proper expedition team. Experienced guides and Sherpa climbing staff play and important role during the expedition. Thy will help with route understanding, preparation to climb, logistics and decision making while in difficult situation while climbing.

When people look for where is Mount Everest, they may imagine the reaching summit as well. In reality, climb requires longer approach followed by more skills and tougher mindset whereas trekking is easier version to see mountain from near and reaching base camp.

The other important thing that need to be considered is acclimatization. Climbers do not rush on climb, rather they will focus on gradual increase in altitude to be familiar with thin air. The usual climbing route from southern side from Nepal includes base camp, and higher camps before the final summit push.

The equipment list for the climbers are also extensive. They need suitable clothing, climbing boots, crampons, harness, helmets, ropes , sleeping equipment’s, supplement oxygen, and other relevant high altitude gear. As we go up, weather can change quickly , so careful planning is essential.

An Everest expedition is not just an ordinary trip and it requires lots of physical preparation, technical climbing and experienced support and careful acclimatization and patience. The summit push come from many days of hard work , preparation, waiting suitable weather, and adapting to the highly demanding himalayan environment of Khumbu region.

Best Time to Visit Mount Everest

This depends upon what do you want to do or expect in the Everest region. Climbing to the summit and trekking to base camp have different seasonal needs. Springs and autumn are considered as main season because the weather is more clear and suitable for mountain views.

Spring is mainly from March to May and its very important for Everest Expeditions. They days are more suitable for climbing. The route will also be busy with trekking during this season as this is main season for trekking and the flower rhododendron add more colors to the lower parts of the trekking route.

Autumn, normally from September to November, is another popular trekking period. After the summer monsoon, the skies are clearer and visible with great views. The route is usually in great condition, making the season great for the travellers who love the views and village.

For people searching where is mount Everest, the visit timing also matters, as spring and autumn could be the more nice and great moment to visit the Everest either for trekking or climbing. Whereas winter bring more cold temperates which make the place hard to trek or do expedition. There may be high snowfall which can affect routes as well. Also, monsoon season can bring more rain, cloud, and less visibility.

The right season depends upon your purpose also, if you want monsoon adventure or snowfall trek then winter or monsoon could be good fit for you but that’s not the demand of general visitor. If you are planning expedition, the season requires much more planning with careful factors and weather forecast and snow condition and safety.

Even in popular season the weather can change fast. Clear morning can turn cloudy and invisible, and strong wind may affect the travel planning. So, good preparation can some flexibility are important when travelling Everest Region.
