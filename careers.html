Our Workplace Ethics

A Culture of Dignity, Fair Pay & Field Safety

We believe that extraordinary guest journeys are forged by crew members who feel safe, respected, and backed by a company that puts mountain human dignity first.

Fair Pay & Porter Welfare

We enforce strict 20kg load limits, provide insulated down clothing and alpine boots, ensure proper lodge sleeping berths, and pay wages well above TAAN minimum guidelines.

High-Altitude Medical & Rescue

Every guide, assistant, and porter is fully insured against altitude illness, frostbite, and emergencies, backed by 24/7 direct satellite emergency dispatch and helicopter evacuations.

Sponsored Training & Growth

Eco-Trekking & Conservation

Lead groups with pride under Leave No Trace standards. We spearhead seasonal trail cleanup drives in the Khumbu and Annapurna sanctuaries and invest in local trail health.

Our Ethical Commitment

The Nepal Unique Treks Porter & Crew Charter

Porters and guides are the true backbone of Himalayan exploration. We do not cut corners on their welfare. Our operational code strictly adheres to the standards of the International Porter Protection Group (IPPG) and the Trekking Agencies' Association of Nepal (TAAN).

  • Mandatory digital luggage weigh-in before commencing any trek
  • Guaranteed warm outerwear, insulated boots, sunglasses, and gloves
  • Sick crew members receive immediate treatment and descend safely with escort
Recognized & Certified By
Certified ethical trekking operator adhering to Nepal Ministry of Culture, Tourism and Civil Aviation standards.
