Frequently Asked Questions

Still have something on your mind? Let's put that to rest!

What are the Legal & Permit Considerations for Trekking in Nepal?▼

Permit requirements depend on your route and destination. Some areas require permits arranged in advance, and rules can change. Check the latest requirements for your itinerary before travel; our team can help you identify which permits may apply.

How can I prepare for a safer high-altitude trek?▼

Choose an itinerary with appropriate pacing, follow local guidance, and discuss fitness or health concerns with a qualified medical professional before departure. Weather and trail conditions can change, so stay flexible and follow your guide's safety instructions.

What should I check when booking with a local trekking operator?▼

Before booking, confirm the operator's registration, what is included in the trip price, guide and porter arrangements, safety procedures, and how itinerary changes or emergencies are handled. Ask for these details in writing so you can compare options clearly.

What travel and medical insurance should I arrange?▼

Review your policy carefully and make sure it covers your planned activities and maximum trekking altitude. If you need emergency evacuation coverage, verify that it is explicitly included and understand the claims process before leaving home.

How do I choose the right trek in Nepal?▼

Consider your available time, fitness, preferred scenery, comfort level, and experience at altitude. Compare itineraries and daily walking demands, then ask for advice if you are unsure which route suits you.

What is the best season for trekking?▼

Spring and autumn are popular trekking seasons, but conditions differ by region and altitude. Check the seasonal weather and trail conditions for your specific route before choosing dates.

How many days should I plan for a trek?▼

Trip length depends on the route, your starting point, acclimatization needs, and travel connections. Include buffer days when possible to allow for weather or transport delays.

Can a trek be adjusted to my pace or interests?▼

Many itineraries can be discussed around your interests, schedule, and comfort preferences. Share your priorities when you inquire so the team can explain which changes are practical for the route.

What is altitude sickness (AMS)?▼

Acute Mountain Sickness can affect people at elevation, and symptoms may worsen if a person ascends further. Learn the signs before your trek, tell your guide if you feel unwell, and seek qualified medical advice about personal risks and prevention.

How can I reduce altitude-related risks?▼

Plan a gradual itinerary with time to acclimatize, avoid rushing to higher elevations, and follow your guide's advice. Ask a healthcare professional about altitude risks and any medication before your trip.

What should I do if I feel unwell at altitude?▼

Tell your guide or trekking companions promptly and follow professional medical guidance. Do not continue ascending while symptoms are present; urgent or worsening symptoms require immediate assistance.

Do I need prior high-altitude trekking experience?▼

Not every trek requires previous high-altitude experience, but routes vary in difficulty and elevation. Choose a trek that matches your preparation and discuss your experience and health considerations with a qualified professional.

Can I request a tailor-made itinerary?▼

Yes. Share your preferred destinations, travel dates, group size, interests, and comfort preferences through our custom trip planning page to start a conversation about your itinerary.

What details should I include in my trip inquiry?▼

Include your approximate dates, number of travelers, destinations or activities of interest, preferred trip length, and any important needs. This helps the team understand what you are looking for.

Can I combine trekking and cultural tours?▼

You can ask about combining trekking with sightseeing or cultural experiences. What is feasible depends on your dates, travel connections, and the time available.
