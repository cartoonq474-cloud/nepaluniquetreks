Year-Round Travel

Best Time of Year for Nepal Tours

Unlike high alpine passes which close during extreme snow, cultural and wildlife tours operate comfortably 12 months a year across Nepal.

Autumn (Sep – Nov)

Peak Crystal Season

Temps: 15°C – 25°C • Clear Skies

The golden window for touring: crisp mountain views, vibrant Dashain and Tihar Hindu festivals, and perfect dry weather for both jungle safaris and helicopter excursions.

Spring (Mar – May)

Wildflowers & Wildlife

Temps: 18°C – 28°C • Warm & Sunny

Rhododendrons and jacarandas bloom across valley hillsides. Long sunny days and pleasant evenings make walking through ancient Durbar squares and boating on Phewa Lake delightful.

Winter (Dec – Feb)

Crisp Days & Quiet Sights

Temps: 8°C – 20°C • Sunny Midday

Clear blue skies with zero humidity. Chitwan wildlife viewing is at its absolute prime as tall grasses recede. Heritage sights are peaceful and free of peak-season crowds.

Monsoon (Jun – Aug)

Lush Green Terraces

Temps: 22°C – 30°C • Rain Showers

Emerald rice paddies, dramatic cloud formations, and exclusive boutique hotel luxury at off-peak rates. Ideal for deep temple meditation, culinary workshops, and cultural discovery.

Practical Information

Essential Nepal Tour Travel Advice

Helpful tips to make your guided tour across Nepal comfortable, seamless, and culturally respectful.

Nepal Tourist Visa on Arrival

Visas are easily obtained upon arrival at Tribhuvan International Airport (KTM) in Kathmandu. 15 Days ($30 USD), 30 Days ($50 USD), or 90 Days ($125 USD). Ensure your passport has at least 6 months validity.

Temple Etiquette & Dress

When visiting holy Hindu and Buddhist shrines like Pashupatinath and Swayambhunath, dress modestly covering shoulders and knees. Remove shoes and leather accessories before entering temple inner sanctums.

Chitwan Safari Packing

Neutral earth-toned clothing (khaki, olive, beige) is strongly recommended for jungle safaris to avoid startling wildlife. Don't forget binoculars, high-SPF sunscreen, and insect repellent.
