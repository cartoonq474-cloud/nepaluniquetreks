The Non-Negotiable 6,000m Altitude Clause Trap

Standard travel policies (including credit card inclusions and general holiday insurance) almost always contain strict altitude exclusions capped at 2,500m or 3,000m. In the Himalayas, standard policies are completely void. Your certificate must explicitly state Emergency Medical Helicopter Evacuation up to 6,000 meters (or higher for peak climbs).

Standard / Credit Card Policy
Capped at 2,500m – 3,000m (Denied)

Helicopter operators will NOT fly without an immediate personal cash deposit or credit card pre-authorization of $3,000 to $5,000 USD. Hospital admission fees are billed directly to you.

Specialized High-Altitude Policy
Explicit 6,000m Evacuation (Approved)

Direct billing guarantee issued immediately to Kathmandu flight operations. Helicopter launches rapidly with $0 personal cash liability, plus direct admission to CIWEC or Era Hospital.

5 Non-Negotiable Insurance Pillars You MUST Have

Before paying your insurance premium, inspect your policy schedule and verify that these five exact provisions are written into your schedule of benefits:

1. High-Altitude Helicopter Search & Rescue up to 6,000m

Mandatory

Immediate emergency airlift from remote mountain landing zones (e.g., Gorakshep, Pheriche, Thorong Phedi, Samagaun) directly to Kathmandu airport. Your policy must not restrict search and rescue to "within 20km of paved roads."

2. Inpatient Hospitalization & Western-Standard ICU Care

Mandatory

Guarantees direct cashless admission and ICU care at internationally accredited Kathmandu hospitals (CIWEC Hospital and Era International Hospital). Must cover high-altitude pulmonary edema (HAPE), cerebral edema (HACE), oxygen therapy, and specialist physician fees.

3. Medically Escorted Repatriation & Emergency Flight Home

Mandatory

Covers medical repatriation to your country of permanent residence, including international business-class stretcher flights with an onboard flight doctor or nurse if severe recovery is required. Also covers repatriation of mortal remains.

4. Mountain Weather Flight Delays & Trip Interruption

Recommended

Himalayan mountain airstrips like Lukla (Tenzing-Hillary) and Jomsom are weather-dependent. Cloud cover frequently delays domestic STOL aircraft for 2 to 3 days. Trip interruption reimburses contingency hotel nights, helicopter diversion shuttles, and rebooked international flights.

5. Delayed, Damaged or Lost Trekking Gear

Recommended

Provides immediate cash allowance to rent or purchase essential replacement alpine gear (down sleeping bags, 800-fill jackets, thermal boots) in Kathmandu’s Thamel market if international airlines mishandle your transit luggage.

How Helicopter Evacuation Works in Nepal (Step-by-Step)

Nepal Unique Treks maintains an active, 24/7 mountain emergency response protocol with civil aviation helicopter charter operators:

1

Clinical Assessment on Trail & Lake Louise Score

Trail Triage (0–30 Mins)

Your licensed Sherpa guide assesses your vitals using a fingertip pulse oximeter (oxygen saturation SpO2 and resting heart rate) and evaluates your Lake Louise Altitude Sickness Score. If severe AMS, pulmonary edema (HAPE), cerebral edema (HACE), or orthopedic trauma is identified, emergency evacuation is authorized immediately.

2

Kathmandu Ops Dispatch & Insurance Direct Clearance

Ops Liaison (30–60 Mins)

Your guide contacts our 24/7 Kathmandu Operations Desk via satellite communicator (Garmin inReach) or mobile network. Our office immediately contacts your international insurance emergency hotline with the clinical report and flight coordinates to secure the payment guarantee letter.

3

Airbus H125 Helicopter Launch & Mountain Extraction

Flight Time (45–90 Mins)

An Airbus H125 rescue helicopter launches from Kathmandu or Lukla. The pilot lands at the nearest village helipad or open alpine meadow, loads the patient under oxygen therapy, and flies directly back to Kathmandu Tribhuvan International Airport.

4

Tarmac Ambulance Transfer & Western Hospital Admission

Medical Care (Immediate)

A dedicated ambulance meets the helicopter directly on the domestic tarmac and transfers you without delay to CIWEC Hospital or Era International Hospital. A member of Nepal Unique Treks management coordinates all paperwork, hospital billing, and keeps your family informed.
