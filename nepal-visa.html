Tribhuvan International Airport (KTM)

Arrival Walkthrough & Electronic Kiosks

Navigating immigration at Kathmandu Airport is stress-free when you follow these sequential stations inside the arrival terminal.

Recommended

⚡ Option A: Online Pre-Registration

Total Time: ~10 to 15 Minutes at Airport

Submit your details on the official nepaliport.immigration.gov.np website within 14 days before your flight. Print out the confirmation barcode receipt. Upon landing, you skip the massive kiosk terminal queues entirely and head straight to the bank payment desk!

🖥️ Option B: Airport Kiosk Touchscreens

Total Time: ~35 to 60 Minutes Queue

Use one of the electronic touchscreen terminals located in the arrival hall. You must scan your passport and take an automated camera photo on-site. When multiple wide-body international flights land together, kiosk lines can back up significantly.

1

Online Pre-Registration (14 Days Before Flight)

At Home (5 Mins)

Visit the official Nepal Immigration portal: nepaliport.immigration.gov.np. Select "Visa On Arrival", fill out your passport data, flight details, and Kathmandu hotel address, upload a passport photo, and print the confirmation page with the QR/barcode.

Pro Tip: Keep the printed barcode slip folded inside your passport carry-on. Do not store it inside checked baggage!
2

Enter the KTM Arrival Hall

Station 1

After deplaning via the aerobridge or shuttle bus, enter the main international immigration hall. If you already have your printed barcode from Station 1, proceed directly past the electronic kiosks toward the bank counters. If you haven't filled it online, queue at an electronic kiosk to register.

3

Pay Fee at Rastriya Banijya Bank Counter

Station 2

Present your printed barcode slip to the Rastriya Banijya Bank teller desks located directly alongside the kiosk area. Pay your designated fee ($30 for 15 days, $50 for 30 days, or $125 for 90 days). The teller will issue your stamped bank payment receipt.

Cash Alert: Hand over exact cash in crisp US Dollars or Euros. Card readers at the bank desk occasionally disconnect due to telecom gateway lags.
4

Immigration Officer Review & Passport Stamp

Station 3

Walk to the designated "Visa on Arrival" immigration lines (differentiated from diplomats, foreigners with visas, and Nepalese nationals). Present your passport, bank payment receipt, and barcode slip. The immigration officer verifies your information, affixes the official sticker visa, and stamps your entry date.

5

Baggage Reclaim & Nepal Unique Treks Welcome

Exit Terminal

Take the escalator down to the baggage claim hall to collect your backpacks. Pass customs green channel. Immediately outside the arrival doors, our Nepal Unique Treks airport representative will greet you holding a personalized name placard and transfer you via private AC vehicle to your hotel in Thamel.

Airport SIM & ATM: Ncell and Nepal Telecom have official tourist SIM card counters right next to the baggage exit, and ATMs are available inside the terminal.

Must possess at least 6 months remaining validity from your date of arrival and at least 2 blank visa pages for stickers and entry/exit stamps.

📄 Printed Barcode Confirmation Slip (nepaliport.immigration.gov.np) Recommended

Filled online within 14 days of departure. Having the printed physical copy saves you 30-45 minutes in airport terminal lines.

💵 Exact Visa Fee in Crisp Cash ($30, $50, or $125 USD) Mandatory

Clean, unmarked, untorn banknotes issued after 2013. Euros, British Pounds, and AUD are also accepted at the bank counter.

You are required to enter your hotel name, district (e.g., Thamel, Kathmandu), and phone number on the immigration declaration form.

Proof of confirmed return flight ticket or onward journey out of Nepal. Immigration officers periodically request to view this.

📸 2 Physical Passport-Sized Photos (2x2 Inches) Backup

While electronic kiosks take a digital snapshot, carrying 2 physical photos ensures seamless backup if kiosks are offline, and they are needed for TIMS & national park permits!

Staying Longer

Extending Your Tourist Visa in Nepal

Planning an additional trek, a meditation retreat, or delayed by mountain weather? Extending your tourist visa inside Nepal is straightforward.

Official Locations

Where to Apply in Nepal

1. Kathmandu: Department of Immigration, Kalikasthan, Dillibazar, Kathmandu. (Open Sun–Thu 10:00–17:00, Fri 10:00–15:00).

2. Pokhara: Immigration Office, Gairapatan, Pokhara (convenient for Annapurna and Dhaulagiri trekkers).

Fees & Duration

Extension Fee Structure

• Minimum Extension: 15 Days for USD $45 (payable in equivalent Nepali Rupees NPR).
• Additional Days: USD $3 per day for every day beyond 15 days.
• Annual Maximum Limit: Foreign tourists can stay up to 150 days per calendar year (Jan 1 to Dec 31).

🚨

Overstay Penalties & Fines

Never overstay your visa! If you overstay without official extension, immigration imposes a fine of USD $5 per day plus the standard late visa extension charge ($3/day). Travelers with overstays exceeding 30 days face mandatory court review and multi-year entry bans. Always file your extension before your current visa expires.

Overland Crossings

Nepal Land Border Immigration Checkpoints

Entering Nepal overland by bus, train, or private vehicle from India or Tibet? You can obtain your visa on arrival at these official immigration checkpoints:

India Border (Central/Lumbini)

Belahiya (Sunauli)

Near Bhairahawa / Gorakhpur. The most popular overland crossing for travelers visiting Lumbini (Birthplace of Buddha) and traveling toward Pokhara.

India Border (Central/Trade)

Birgunj (Raxaul)

Connecting Bihar with central Nepal. Direct overland road and commercial highway leading straight up to Kathmandu Valley.

India Border (Eastern)

Kakarbhitta (Panitanki)

Connecting Eastern Nepal with Siliguri, Darjeeling, and Sikkim. Ideal for travelers coming from North-East India or West Bengal.

India Border (Western)

Jamunaha (Nepalgunj)

Near Lucknow. Gateway for remote western trekking (Dolpo, Rara Lake) and overland spiritual pilgrimages toward Mount Kailash.

India Border (Far-West)

Gaddachauki (Mahendranagar)

Connecting with Uttarakhand (Banbasa / New Delhi). Ideal for road journeys originating in Delhi or northern India.

Tibet / China Border

Rasuwagadhi (Kyirong)

Connecting Langtang district with Tibet Autonomous Region. Open for group visa holders traveling between Lhasa and Kathmandu.
